# Hoth's ground: the game's snow over the record's colour

2026-10-12. The site's Hoth ground was two heightmap grids under one flat `MeshStandardMaterial`: it looked like a sheet. The owner's study of the game's terrain (`bf2-re/findings/35-terrain-layers.md`) read the base snow layer's shader: no colour map, five normal maps at fixed tilings, a brightness map, a soot mask, a sparkle map on the near pass, blowing-snow streaks, and a displacement near the camera over one engine-fed colour (the clamp decision takes the record's `TerrainColor`). The ground now draws that layer. The pictures are in `bf2017-ground-2026-10-12/`.

**In one sentence:** the ground is a TSL `MeshStandardNodeMaterial` (`src/lib/three/ground/snow.js`, the record in `snowMaps.js`) that sums `T_ArcticBase_Snow_02_N` at 4 m and 33 m with `T_ArcticBase_SnowDetail_01_N` at 1 m in world xz over the record's colour 0.547 / 0.594 / 0.644, with smoothness 0.26 moved by `SnowSmoothnessVirtualTexture_01_RGBM`, `T_BlackSootMask_01_C` darkening, `SnowPackedSmoothness_01_RGBM` streaks scrolled along 172° through `T_BlowingSnowFlowMap_01_C`, `SnowSparkle_03_RGBM` as a roughness dip on the near band, and on ultra a 1 m patch round the player whose vertices the 4 m map's blue displaces; it costs 0.8 ms a frame at ultra at 5120 × 1440.

## 1. The textures

Seven maps from the owner's export (`BF2_Extract/web/textures/objects/nature/arctic/_arcticbase/`), encoded by `scripts/bf2017-snow.mjs` through the site's KTX2 path (`scripts/ktx2.mjs`: UASTC, mipmapped, zstd) and written at each tier's size as the level factory writes its rows (the bigger file's mips dropped), with `tex` rows in the pack's `level.json`. The `_N` maps stay packed (R, G the normal, B the height, A a mask) as lane Q2's ground rows are, so one sample gives the shader both; the `_C` maps are sRGB (the game's format is BC7_SRGB), the rest linear.

| `t_arcticbase_snow_02_n` | 2048² BC7 | 256 / 512 / 1024 / 2048 | 57 / 207 / 736 / 2,793 KB |
| `t_arcticbase_snowdetail_01_n` | 512² RGBA8 | 256 / 512 | 33 / 163 KB |

| `t_blowingsnowflowmap_01_c` | 1024² BC7 sRGB | 256 / 512 | 22 / 66 KB |

A tier's download: **mid 0.8 MB, high 3.0 MB, ultra 6.7 MB** (22 files, 9.0 MB over every row); on the GPU at ultra 10.3 MB (BC7, mips). `WORLD_MB['/battlefront']` carries the mid figure.

**What needs the owner's key.** The files are in `public/models/galaxy/bf2017/levels/hoth/tex/` and not in git (the pack's binaries are published, lane E0). `node scripts/assets-publish.mjs --only 'models/galaxy/bf2017/levels/hoth/tex/*'` with `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPA_KEY` sends them to `site-assets` by hash, registers them in `src/data/galaxyAssets.json` and rewrites `.gitignore`'s block. Until then the site's copy serves them in development; on the published site a map the bucket lacks is a 404 the ground reads as that map's miss (never the asset base down), and its term stays out: the flat colour as before.

## 2. The material (`src/lib/three/ground/snow.js`)

| term | the game (finding 35) | the site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| colour | `cb1[13]`, engine-fed; the record's `TerrainColor` | the record's colour, flat |
| normal | `Snow_02_N` @0.25/m + `Snow_02_N` @0.03/m + `SnowDetail_01_N` @1/m, summed | the same three reads in world xz, summed under the geometry normal (UDN); the 1 m octave fades over 30–80 m |
| smoothness | 0.26; `SmoothnessVirtualTexture_01_RGBM.r + 0.05` @0.45/m as the grey of a track | 0.26 × 2(r + 0.05), clamped 0.1–0.5 (roughness 1 − s): the map's middle grey leaves 0.26 |
| soot | `BlackSootMask_01_C.r·b > 0.9` @0.2/m | the colour × (1 − 0.45 · smoothstep(0.9, 1, r·b)) |
| blowing snow | two reads of `PackedSmoothness_01_RGBM` at (0.08, 0.04) and (0.02, 0.08) cycles/m, slid along 172°, offset by `BlowingSnowFlowMap_01_C` × 0.8 − 0.1 @0.2/m, `smoothstep(sat(4(a² − a²b² − 0.15)))`, masked to the painted layer | the same reads and combination (`time` × 2.5 m/s); no mask, so at half weight: the colour lifted 6 % toward white, the normal flattened 60 %, smoothness 0.25 |
| sparkle | `SnowSparkle_03_RGBM` on the 3-D (near) pass | its red dips the roughness by up to 0.5 on the near band, so the sun glints on the crystals |
| displacement | the height's blue @0.2/m × 0.35 + 0.4, by `DetailDisplacementScale` | on ultra: the 4 m map's blue at that tiling, centred, × 0.35 m, on the 1 m patch's vertices (`textureSampleLevel` in the vertex stage) |

Tiers: low the colour alone, no map fetched; mid the 4 m and 1 m octaves (two samples); high every map (nine samples: three normals, the brightness, the soot, the flow and two streak reads, the sparkle); ultra the same plus the patch. The far grid (32 m) reads the two large octaves alone on every tier from mid.

The patch (`src/components/battlefront/map/ground.js`): 300 m a side at 1 m (90,601 vertices, the heightmap's own 1 m pixels), built round the player and again once they have walked 60 m; the 4 m grid drops 0.3 m under it so the two never fight. The game's base-layer masks (which of its eight layers lies where) are still undecoded: this is layer 0 everywhere; lane Q2's derived masks and per-layer maps (`layeredGround.js`) remain the galaxy's `/roam` ground.

## 3. The measurement

Headless Edge on the RTX 5090 (`--use-angle=d3d11`), 2560 × 720 at device scale 2 (5120 × 1440), `tp-quality=ultra`, `?gpu=webgpu`, deployed as the assault class and advanced 30 s; `world.draw` wrapped as `.scratch-check/bf-profile2.mjs` does, the draw's median ms over 3 s. One session, six paired rounds alternating the ground's materials (the snow as built, then the same meshes under a plain node material with the patch hidden: the ground as it was), the order swapped each round so the machine's drift falls on both sides.

| round | snow p50 | flat p50 | difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 46.4 | 46.3 | +0.1 |
| 2 | 45.7 | 47.2 | −1.5 |
| 3 | 50.4 | 49.6 | +0.8 |
| 4 | 49.9 | 47.4 | +2.5 |
| 5 | 42.1 | 44.4 | −2.3 |
| 6 | 51.9 | 48.5 | +3.4 |

**The ground adds 0.8 ms a frame at ultra** (the median of the paired differences; the mean +0.5; the spread −2.3 … +3.4 on a machine running other sessions, whose whole frame sat at 42–52 ms through the runs). Under the 1.5 ms asked. Nothing in the console.

## 4. The pictures

- `hoth-field-flat.webp` / `hoth-field-snow.webp`: the field ahead of the deploy, before and after. The sheet is now a field: the 33 m swells, the 4 m lumps, the 1 m grain, the streaks lying across it.
- `hoth-near-flat.webp` / `hoth-near-snow.webp`: the ground at the soldier's feet, the same crop. The near pixel means match (209.7 / 214.9 / 220.1 against 210.6 / 215.8 / 220.9): the record's colour and its exposure are as the clamp decision left them.
- `hoth-down-snow.webp`: looking down at the field from the hip. It clips to white at the record's clamp, exactly as the flat ground did (the crop's mean 240 either way): not the material's doing, and the next thing to read with the sky's values (the clamp decision's "what is left").
- `game-drive-12-snow.webp`: the owner's capture of the game's snow (`bf2-re/data/drive-12.png`, the hangar mouth), cropped to the ground. The game's is trodden, lumpy at the half-metre, blue-white under the hangar's lights; the site's field has the grain and the swells but reads flatter near the feet, for want of the packed and trampled layers' masks and the game's displacement scale.

## 5. What is left

- The eight paint masks (the streaming tree's 72 × 72 tiles) and the engine's `DetailDisplacementScale`: with them the packed, trampled and rocky layers draw where the game draws them, and the lumps at their true height.
- The near field's clip at EV 15 looking down: the sky's values and `LuminanceScale` (the clamp decision).
- The streaks' rate: the shader's time constant is unread; 2.5 m/s is judged against the capture.
