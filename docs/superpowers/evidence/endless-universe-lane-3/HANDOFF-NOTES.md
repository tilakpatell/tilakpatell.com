# Endless universe, lane 3: hand-off notes

For the coordinator to fold into `docs/superpowers/HANDOFF-universe.md`. PR 3.1 (Tasks 1 to 9, the pure half, pull request 1301) and PR 3.2 (Tasks 10 to 15, the wiring) are both in.

## Landed

- `nav.js` takes generated places through its caller's lookup, `{ place(id) → Destination | null, park(id, from: [x, z]) → Park | null }`, not `{ place }` alone. The park comes from the lookup so the pages that load the nav don't carry `park.js` (with it, ⌘K grew by 2.14 kB gzipped: the README's Task 7). `lookupOf(opts)` in `places/resolve.js` makes one.
- A generated id names a sector at most `FURTHEST` (2^26) off on each axis (`places/ids.js`); past that `isPlaceId` is false and every lookup gives nothing at once. The generator can't lay out a sector past about 4.5e14: its grid cells pass 2^53 and `gridOf().clear` in `expanse/gen/space.js` never ends. So a typed `fly` or a link to such an id can't freeze the page or leave a worker spinning. The generator and its fingerprint are as they were.
- `ship.js`'s `goalAt(id)` reads `GOALS` by its own keys only, and `isGoal`, `parkAt`, nav's `canGo` and `tripTime` go through it. A word on `Object.prototype` (`constructor`, `toString`) is no longer a goal, and `parkFor` and `tripTime` give nothing for it, not a throw.

### PR 3.2, the wiring

- `universe/trips.js`: the scene's travel, moved out of `scene.js` word for word, then taught generated places. `createTrips(deps) → { travel, dropAuto, arriveAt, navTo, frontSpace, withPilot, pilotGone, chasePilot, followPlace, outOfJump, endTrip }`. A generated id in any spelling is gone to at once when the scene's sectors hold it, waited for (`state.pending`) while the pool makes its sector, and nowhere when its sector is at hand without it. Its park follows the place each frame; a jump comes out where it is at the flash; a trip's end is `state.arrived`, a new object each time.
- `universe/places/scenePlaces.js`: the scene's places. `createScenePlaces({ group, universe, expanse, workers, startAt, toScreen, prepare, emit, note, device, tags, onPick, frame })` → `update`, `around`, `carry`, `solids`, `here`, `docked`, `resolve`, `fetch`, `find`, `missing`, `park`, `look` (`{ place, park }`, the lookup `nav.js` takes), `mark`, `name`, `nearby`, `aimTargets`, `pick`, `screen`, `debug`. It reads `expanse.sectors()` by each sector's own id and fetches what isn't loaded from the `'expanse'` pool (`place|<key>`), never making a sector on the main thread. Events `{ type: 'place', what: 'arrive' | 'leave' | 'dock' | 'undock' | 'found' }`.
- `universe/places/stationLook.js`: generated stations, two `InstancedMesh`es (hull and lights), none drawn with no station in view; a station shows only inside its system's reach and grows in from the edge. Solid through `places.solids()` in the frame's `moving`.
- `universe/places/placeTags.js`: the in-flight name tags, laid out by `chartLabels.js`'s `placeLabels` with the frame as its box (the plan named a lane 6a `declutter.js` that doesn't exist). At most `budget.tags`.
- `universe/places/PlaceCard.jsx` and `pageCard.js`: a generated place's card in the panel, at `/universe/E:…`; it plots while the scene finds it and, with no scene (3D off, failed or lost), shows the id and how to get there at once. The page never resolves an id.
- `universe/budget.js` gains `tags` (3 on low, 6 above, stepped with the discs). `expanse/shared/gen.js` lends `sectorJob` and `withMoons`.
- `scene.js`: the handle gains `place(id)`, `fetchPlace(id)` and `nearby(max)`; `where()` gains `place`, `docked` and `t`; `__universe()` gains `t`, `place` and `docked`; `__universeDebug.places` is `places.debug` (`resolve`, `parkAt`, `screen`, `stations`). A click on a generated place flies there; a link to one starts there (by the jump once its sector lands). Docking at the end of a trip opens the Hangar on its shipyard tab, once.
- The terminal's `fly E:…` makes its one sector on its own page (214 ms longest task at 4× CPU throttling, in software GL).
- `poses.js`: `expanse-station`, parked at `E:1,0`'s first station, made only when asked; `universe-check.mjs` measures it with `--poses`.
- `scripts/universe-endless-check.mjs`: `--only`, and the steps `travel` (two systems and a moving planet, carried 30 s), `dock` (with the Hangar), `pick`, `link`, `flat` and `terminal`.

## Left

- **The lane 3 plan's "What later lanes consume" table is corrected** (its `nav.js` row, which lanes 6b, 4c and 5b read): `parkFor` and `tripTime` take the lookup's `park(id, from)` as well as `place`. A lookup made by hand with only `place` gives a null park, so a dash for every generated trip, with no error. `lookupOf` and the scene's `places.look` supply both.
- **A ship parked off a moon can be hit by a sibling moon.** `parkPlace` keeps a planet's park clear of its moons, but a moon's park isn't checked against the planet's other moons. Carried at a fixed offset from its moon, the ship sweeps a copy of that moon's circle round the planet, which a sibling's circle can cross. Over `E:1,0`, from four ways in (6,264 moon parks, 600 s each, by a scratch test that isn't committed): 4 are hit by a sibling and 8 come within `clearOf` of one. For example, parked at `E:1,0:35:0:1` from +x, `E:1,0:35:0:0` (orbits 158 and 142, the park 26 off) comes within 9.7 of the ship at t ≈ 85 s, inside its radius of 12.1. Scoring the 32 ways on it, as a planet's are, would clear only 3 of the 8: in the other 5 no level way is clear, because the two moons' orbits are closer than the park's distance and `clearOf` together. In `E:1,0:35:0` they are 15.8 apart, under the two moons' radii together (23.9), so the moons themselves can pass through each other where their circles cross. That spacing is the generator's (`moonsOf` in `expanse/gen/systems.js`). A fix needs a park off the moons' planes, or moons spaced by their radii.
- **Lane 6b** lists generated places on the chart and in search from `handle.nearby()`, `where().t` and `findDestinations`' `more`, passing `{ place: handle.place }` to `nav.js`. A visited place outside the loaded sectors comes from `handle.fetchPlace(id)`, one worker build a sector (about 60 ms in Node): resolve the ones in view, not the whole list. Generated systems are `kind: 'star'`; `KINDS`' "Star systems" is `'system'` (the galaxy's gates), so fold `'star'` into that group.
- **Lane 4a** draws its kinds' cards (`THING_CARDS`) and counts the station as catalogue kind 11; it may restyle `stationLook.js`.
- **Lane 4b**'s trader event can hail from a station's dock (the `'place'` `dock` event).
- **Lane 5b** lands from `where().place` (a planet whose card has `land: true`) and returns to `/universe/E:…`, which now starts parked at the place with its card up.
- **Anyone importing from `places/`** from a module that loads with a page (`nav.js` and what it reaches, the page, the panel) takes ids and `atTime` from `places/ids.js` and orbits from `expanse/shared/orbits.js`, never `places/resolve.js`, `places/cards.js` or `expanse/shared/gen.js`. `weight.test.js` fails otherwise.
- `places/cards.js` holds `FAR` (45,000) itself: lane 2's `levels.js` exports the same but sits under `expanse/scene/`, which the universe may not import. `ids.js` and `around.js` copy `orbitAt`'s answer into their own arrays: lane 2's `orbitAt` makes a new one each call.
