# The cast, mapped: Battlefront II (2017), phase 2

Every kind the worlds place (`node scripts/galaxy-figures-audit.mjs --json`, 102 kinds), and the plan’s new ones, against what the game has. Each row names the game’s body and parts by their exact manifest names (`lab/assets/bf2017/web/models.jsonl`); every name in this file was checked against it, and `cast.json` beside this file is the same table for scripts.

Of the 102 audited kinds, 39 take the game’s files, 8 are done (phase 1’s heroes, and `kenobi`, which is a question), and 55 stay as they are. The plan adds 20 kinds, and PR #795’s Hoth people are mapped at the end in case it merges (none of them is placed by a world today).

## How to read it

- **rig**: `walrus` is the game’s human skeleton (`Characters/Rigs/Humanoids/Walrus_HumanMale`, 4,498 clips in `anims.jsonl`). “Walrus by name” is a skeleton of its own whose every bone carries a walrus bone’s name (checked on the GLBs), so the walrus pack can drive it by name; none of these has the weapon sockets (`Wep_Root`, `Wep_Muzzle`, `Wep_Aim`, `IK_Joint_*`), so `checkWalrus` fails them and the import needs `--no-walrus-check` or a check without sockets. “Own” is a skeleton with its own clips.
- **LOD triangles**: the body’s chain, LOD0 first. Where parts and a head go with it, the whole figure’s LOD0 is in brackets (body, parts and the first head).
- **parts and heads**: what the game’s outfit record puts on the body. A body that stops at the neck (its top near 1.6 m) needs a head or a helmet; that is said in the row.
- **outfit variations**: the game’s own, from its records (below), by the record’s name, with the files that differ from the row’s. Most are material variations: the same mesh with other textures or other marking colours.
- **fielded**: the kinds `assaults.js`’s sides and `troops.js`’s `TROOPS` put in squads. They need a `.far` cut.

## Where the variations come from

The records were read, not guessed. The bucket’s `data.tsv` (the index of `data/`, 83,983 rows) lists 3,610 MeshVariationDatabase and 2,807 ObjectVariation records. Fetched with the service key (a script in the scratchpad that never prints it) and read:

- **The outfit records**: each multiplayer class’s `VisualUnlockRootAsset` (`data/Characters/<Light|Dark>/<class>/<outfit>/MPVUR_<outfit>.json.gz`) and its bundles’ MeshVariationDatabases (`data/Characters/…/<outfit>/mpvur_<outfit>_bpb/MeshVariationDb_Win32.json.gz`, one per skin), 1,143 of them. Each lists the meshes an outfit wears and the variation on each. The index gives these paths in lower case; the bucket keeps the folder’s own case and the bundle’s lower case, so the folder has to be matched without case.
- **The marking and colour variations**: 656 ObjectVariations under `data/Characters/` (the clone legions under `Characters/Markings/<104|212|212_Desert|327|41|41Green|41Scout|501|91|CGuard|KASH|THEE>/`, the droids’ under `Characters/Markings/D_Preq/`, the civilians’ under `Characters/NPC/Humans/Civ_MosEisley/`, `Civ_Theed/` and `_Shared/`). These hold colours (a legion’s `Markings_Color`, a civilian’s `ColorClothes` and `ColorHairSkin`, an index into a palette texture); the texture swaps are in the MeshVariationDatabase entry beside them.
- **The levels’ records**: the multiplayer maps’ MeshVariationDatabases (`data/Levels/MP/<Map>/<layer>/MeshVariationDb_Win32.json.gz`, 27 read), which say which character meshes and variations each map loads (the Mos Eisley and Theed civilians’ five and four palettes, the R4’s R4A22 skin on Tatooine).
- **The campaign’s Vardos civilians**: `data/Characters/NPC/Humans/SP_Civ_Vardos/VUR_SP_Civ_Vardos_<Male|Female>_0N.json.gz`, each a whole outfit (robe, coat, trousers, boots, hands, head, hair).

One thing the import must know: the variations’ own textures are mostly not in the bucket yet. `web/textures/` holds the maps the manifest’s models name, and only those: no Yavin rebel (`T_L_Assault_Orig_YA_01_CS`), no Mos Eisley helmet (`T_D_Assault_Orig_MOS_01_Helmet_CS`), no brown Torso_01, no clone marking masks (`L_Preq_T_*`), no civilian palettes (`T_Civ_*_P`). Until they are uploaded those variations cannot be built, and a sandtrooper’s helmet is a stormtrooper’s.

## The plan’s new kinds

The legions are the clone’s body and a helmet with the legion’s markings (a mask texture and a colour), not four helmets: the four helmet meshes are Phase II (`_01`), Phase I (`_02`), the 91st’s (`_03`) and the 212th’s desert one (`_04`). Kamino’s default in the game is the plain Phase I, which is `clonephase1`; so `clonekamino` and `clonephase1` are one figure, and only one of them should be made. The officers and droids are for the Death Star interior’s cast (`inside/pack.js`) and Scarif; the villager pool is `civdesert1..3`, `civtheed1..2` and `civcity1..3`.

## What the import should know

- **Draw calls**: the rebels’ outfits are modular. The Hoth trooper is six meshes with six materials (torso, legs, gloves, backpack, helmet, head), the Endor rebel seven, the Mos Eisley rebel six, the Vardos civilians five to seven. `--join` per material leaves as many draw calls as materials, so these cannot reach four without an atlas or fewer parts. The single-mesh troopers (stormtrooper full body, scout, shore, death trooper) and the crowd civilians are one or two.
- **Heads**: the rebels’ and the officers’ bodies stop at the neck. A head is about 11,000 triangles at LOD0 (Hakan, Charlie, Henry, Nils), as much as a crowd civilian’s whole body, so the head is the figure’s biggest single cost after the torso.
- **Civilians are crowd figures**: the Mos Eisley and Theed people are 6,000 to 8,000 triangles, 48 bones, one material coloured from a palette by the variation’s two numbers. Mos Eisley 01 wears Theed 01’s textures with a desert palette. Theed’s crowd agents (about 500 triangles, unrigged) are a ready far stand-in.
- **No helmet for the shadowtrooper**: the game’s record names the body alone, and the body stops at the neck.
- **One LOD only**: `d_navycrewman_orig_01_mesh`, `sp_imperial_admiral_orig_02_mesh` and `pilot_xwing_orig_01_mesh` have no chain; the stormtrooper’s full body has four.
- **Heavy bodies**: C-3PO (109,841), the bantha (71,778), the death trooper (66,575) and the gamorrean (54,768) at LOD0.
- **Left out as sequel-era**: every `newera`, First Order and Resistance outfit, the BB units, the First Order interrogation and patrol droids, `Civ_Jakku`, `Civ_Takodana` and Starkiller. Vardos is kept: it is the campaign’s Imperial city after Endor, not the sequel films.
