# The game's in-game UI: where it lives, what it draws, what the site now draws

2026-10-12. The owner asked for the game's UI, the map above all, found in the export and the important parts ported. This page is the finding and the port; the shots are in `bf2017-hud/`. The menus (Start → Play → the mode cards → loading → spawn) are another lane's (`claude/bf-menus-lag`) and are not touched here.

## 1. Where the game's in-game UI lives

Everything is under `C:\Users\tilak\Downloads\BF2_Extract\web\data\UI\InGame\`, one JSON a Frostbite blueprint, each a `UIWidgetBlueprint` whose `objects[]` are the elements (`*ElementData`: Text, Rectangle, VectorShape, RegularPolygon, Svg, StackingContainer…) with `Anchor` (a share of the parent and of itself), `Size`, `Offset`, `PaletteIndex` (into `UI/Styles/Colors/UIColorPalette`, 61 colours), `FontStyle` (`UI/Styles/Fonts/Univers520MediumCondensed18px` and so on) and the logic graph beside them (`*EntityData`, hashed field names). The site's lane 0 already flattened the widgets into `src/data/bf2017/ui.json` (88 widget trees, the palette, the fonts), the vector shapes into `src/data/bf2017/shapes.json` (292, every crosshair, hexagon, radar and health shape) and the icons into the sprites under `public/ui/bf2017/` (`src/data/bf2017/icons.json`, 626 names). The fonts the HUD names are all in `public/fonts/bf2017/` (`scripts/bf2017-ui.mjs fonts`, loaded by `src/lib/bf2017/fonts.css` as `BF Univers Condensed` by grade and `BF RaxusPrime`); the files are sound TrueType (`0001 0000`). The "OTS parsing error" came from `hud/battlefront.css` asking for `/battlefront/fonts/*.ttf`, a folder that does not exist (a 404's HTML served as a font); the HUD now imports `fonts.css` and reads its families, so the game's faces load (`check.json`: `BF Univers Condensed` 400/500/700/800 loaded). The menus lane keeps the pipeline.

The owner's captures (`bf2-re/data/drive-11.png`, `drive-12.png`) are Blast on Hoth's hangar: the two score pills with "DEFEAT THE IMPERIALS" under them, the kill log at the top right ("TK-523 defeated Aran Gunnar", the names by side), the octagonal radar at the bottom left, "150 ▬▬▬ ⬡2" at the bottom middle, the three hexagons with E over the middle one and Q / F under the outer ones at the bottom right, the three-prong crosshair in the middle.
