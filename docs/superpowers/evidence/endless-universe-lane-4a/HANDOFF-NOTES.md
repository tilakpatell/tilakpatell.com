# Lane 4a’s hand-off notes: things in space and gargantuans

For the coordinator to fold into `docs/superpowers/HANDOFF-universe.md`.

## Landed

- **4a.1, the catalogue in the generator** (#1312). `expanse/gen/catalogue.js`: the spec’s 23 entries drawn as 32 kinds, each with its look, its far-layer disc, what’s solid and whether it may sit in the core. The lattice has eight kinds (lane 2’s four, then outposts, beacons, relays and monoliths), each thing’s traits from its own seed (`traitsOf`). Sixteen sector lists (`expanse/gen/extras/`): the gargantuan (w3), the arrival’s (w4, lane 4c’s), a wormhole (w8), remnants, pulsars and magnetars, forming stars, ion storms, nebula pockets, swarms, leviathan herds and debris (w10–w45, none in the core), and round the systems fields, comets, rigs, fleets and shipyards (w20000 +, w30000 + … w60000 +). Binary and trinary systems (`companions.js`). Movers on the shared clock (`paths.js`). Gargantuans (`gargantuans.js`): one in exactly one sector of every 3 × 3 × 3 (three residues mod 3, never the core), five forms, 20 to 200 planets across, the far plane and their size off every hand-made place. Lane 3’s places take every kind (cards, words, parks; disc-less rows listed outside the cap; a companion picks its system; a comet carries a parked ship). The lattice keeps 31 of its sizes off every keep-out (`THING_CLEAR`), so no baseline pose has one built near it.
- **4a.2, drawn** (#1316). Lane 2’s near and far layers read the catalogue; a gargantuan is far-layer discs at its true angular size out to 400,000 (`expanse/scene/giants.js`, no draw call) and a mesh near (`universe/things/gargantuans.js`); every near kind is drawn through lane 2’s `drawers` hook (`universe/things/shapes.js`, `drawers.js`, `layout.js`), stock materials only. `budget.js`’s `thingKinds` and the gargantuans’ detail by step.
- **4a.3, what you can do** (`universe/things/rules.js`, `index.js`): salvage, ore, scans and lore, a comet’s tail, a beacon’s lead, a hail, all paying `EARN.found`; an ion storm’s drain (no hit); a pulsar’s beam scrambling the HUD; a wormhole’s one-way throw (`wormholeExit`). `scene.js` four lines in, the cockpit table out to `cabs.js`.
