### Lane D2: every detail (`claude/bf2017-d2-detail`, PR #926)

- **Every pack has its ultra cut.** All seven (hoth, hoth/base, endor, and the four space packs) were rebuilt with `--ultra` and published by hash: LOD0 above 12,000 triangles is in ultra’s row, its maps are up to 2048, and ultra drops nothing but Fondor’s 20 meshes (it takes half the row). E1 to E4: run your packs’ commands again with `--ultra` once this lands.
- **The caps.** The step up is ultra’s alone (2048 / 2048 / 1024, normal and ORM at the colour’s size), held to 256 MB by `fitMaps` (`lod.js`’s `TEX_MB`). High and mid keep main’s 1024 / 512 / 256 with data a step under. As the plan wrote it, high would have fetched about 290 MB on Hoth against the 160 MB bucket row, and the evidence has the sums. If the owner wants high a step up, the bucket row has to move with a reason.
- **Anisotropy.** Pack and crew maps take the tier’s value (16, 4 or 1) through `levelGltf.js`’s `sharpenPack`.
- **Decals.** The parts writer fetches each colour map into `tex/decals/`. Normal-only decals are kept with `texture: null` and not drawn: Endor’s 11 bumped-metal plates need the template’s normal map, a desktop ask.
- **Restored meshes.** `STILL_SKINNED` puts Endor’s 425 wind-bent shrubs in the pack in their bind pose.
- **Probes and LUTs.** On ultra, probes come at 128² with the sun (`<faces>.ultra.<face>.hdr`, `levelProbes.js`’s `facesOf`). Every light record’s LUT is at 33³ (`scripts/bf2017-lut.mjs`; `bf2017-light.mjs` writes 33³ from now on).
- **The gate.** `galaxy-check.mjs` counts every `.glb`, `.ktx2` and `.bin`. A world the bucket served is held to `galaxy-budget.mjs`’s `BUCKET_MB`: main’s Hoth measured with the bucket, +10 % (45 / 70 / 160 / 285 MB).
- **Owners.** The five textures lane W found with no lane are now owned: the terrain’s dynamic decal masks by D2, the depth-of-field shapes by T2, the space set’s cloud normal by the space lane.
- **What never draws.** `NEVER` now also takes light blockers, occluders, invisible collision and shadow meshes (by name; lane E1). `proxyMeshes` leaves out a mesh whose every material is `SS_Shadowmesh` or one of its kin, or `SS_VirtualTextureOnly` (by shader, from `materials.jsonl`; lane E2’s Naboo). Each README lists what stays out. E1 to E5 get both rules on their next run.
- **Low is over its triangle row, counted whole.** Hoth draws 1.39M and Endor 0.93M against 0.8M (Endor’s level is 6.7k of it). The packs are where `fitCull` put them (Hoth’s level is 703k, 90 % of the row); it is the site’s own things that take the rest. The evidence has the numbers and the two ways out: give each pack its `--share` from the world measured without it, or trim the site’s low tier. That is the owner’s call, and no row was raised.
- **Not here: lane E2.** 450 of Naboo’s 2,359 mesh materials carry no textures in `materials.jsonl`, because the surface shader holds them; for 120 of them the shader’s own folder has a `_CS` map (#904).
