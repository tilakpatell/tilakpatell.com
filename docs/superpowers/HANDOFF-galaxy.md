# Hand-off: the galaxy

The Star Wars galaxy at `/galaxy`: a map of systems joined by hyperlanes, flown in a crew’s ship; seventeen walkable worlds under it at `/galaxy/:id/surface`, each with places, zones, quests, people and a ground war; three fleet wars on a shared clock with set pieces at Endor, Hoth and Scarif; the crews’ ship powers and ship-to-ship contact; low flight over endless ground at `/fly/:planet` with a shared, durable world; the inside of both Death Stars at `/deathstar/inside`; and a lit stack (three’s node renderer, TSL and Rapier) under the surfaces, fed by the 2017 game’s own level records. The code is the truth where this file disagrees with it.

## The owner’s rules

- No sequel trilogy (Episodes 7 to 9) anywhere: no figure, place or name. After Episode 6 only The Mandalorian and Ahsoka.
- Every planet has unique things to do, not just “land and walk”. Tatooine is the model to copy.
- Better Luke, Han and crew models. Merge to `main` as pieces finish. Keep output terse.
- The asset upgrade uses no paid service: no Meshy, no Sketchfab API. Its assets come from the assets repo and its releases by URL and from the Quaternius zips; a step that needs a key stops and says so. Elsewhere the owner said to use Meshy where needed, and for stations overrode gen3d-first: Sketchfab CC BY first, then Meshy, then gen3d from a still.
- Compare, never assume: a model replaces a built or shipped one only if it rigs and the sheet says it is better. The Venator and TIE in the assets release are not brought in.
- The surface loaders (`actors.js`, `crew.js`, `placer.js`, `animator.js`, `clipLibrary.js`) are not touched by the asset work; `rig.js` gains only `findBones` and the `_<digits>` rule. Every shipped `ual-*.glb` re-bakes byte for byte; none is regenerated.
- Sizes: a surface model under 2.5 MB (4 MB `hero`); a baked core set under 60 KB a figure; nothing under `lab/` is committed. Credits: Sketchfab in `src/data/modelCredits.json` (its test is the law), CC0 in `public/cc0/README.md`, `CREDITS.md` through `scripts/credits.mjs`.
- Never print or commit the Sketchfab, Meshy or Supabase keys. The client holds Supabase’s publishable key only; the secret key appears nowhere, and a session that finds one uses it for nothing and says so.
