## What was found (measured, 2026-10-10 13:50–14:10 UTC)

### Bug 1: the site never asks the bucket, so the 2017 heroes 404

- The production build (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) passes `VITE_ASSET_BASE: ${{ vars.ASSET_BASE }}`. **The repository has no `ASSET_BASE` variable** (`gh variable list` is empty), so the deployed bundle carries no bucket base at all (the live `index-*.js` has no `storage/v1/object/public` string) and every path in `src/data/galaxyAssets.json` is asked of `tilakpatell.com`.
- Those 371 files are the game-derived ones the bucket alone holds (git ignores them): the heroes' bodies and light cuts (`models/galaxy/bf2017/crew/luke.glb`, `luke.lod1.glb`, `vader.glb`, `vader.lod1.glb`, `clips-vader.glb`, …), the AT-AT's cuts, the effect sheets. On the live Hoth surface they all return **404**. Luke's and Vader's bodies therefore never load, `scene.js`'s `fit` resolves `false`, the page's `hero` event says `ok: false`, and the Loadout panel's toast reads "Didn't load. Still …": that is "it wouldn't let me switch to Luke".
- The bucket itself is fine: `ASSET_BASE=https://jzabcqboyemokwifmjmp.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/site-assets node scripts/assets-check.mjs` → **371 of 371 right**, a year's cache, 12 s.
- **The fix is one repository variable** (`ASSET_BASE` = that URL) and a re-run of the deploy. The architecting session's auto-mode classifier refused to set it ("Production Deploy"), so the owner sets it: GitHub → Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → Variables → New, or `gh variable set ASSET_BASE --body "<url>"`, then `gh workflow run deploy.yml --ref main`. The deploy's `assets-check` step runs first and gates it.
- **Code must still change**: a 2017 hero whose file cannot be fetched must fall back to a body the site has, and say so plainly, instead of refusing the pick. Lane F.

### Bug 2: the landing veil holds at "Sending pictures to the graphics chip 33%"

- Live, on a desktop with an RTX 5090 and WebGPU, `#/galaxy/hoth/surface` stayed at that step for over a minute. The same was seen on two local servers from current `main` (one with the bucket base, one without), but both in **background tabs**, which throttle `requestAnimationFrame`, so that part of the measurement is not trusted; the live observation was in a fronted tab. The owner's report came from a phone.
- Hypotheses for lane F to test, in order: (1) the warm step (`warm(inner)` in `scene.js`'s `kitOut`, and the level pack's upload) awaits something that never resolves when a file 404s; (2) the Hoth level pack's 47 MB of GPU textures at high compiles for a long time with no progress reported between 33 % and the next step; (3) a background or hidden tab (the repo's own note: a hidden browser pane stalls the universe page; `landing-checks`). Decision 8 below says what the veil must do regardless of the cause.

**How a mod becomes site data.** Frosty Mod Manager applies a profile's `.fbmod`s into `<game>\ModData\<Profile>\` (a Data + Patch mirror with the patched bundles; `FrostyModManager.exe -launch "<Profile>"` applies and launches; the game is closed at once). The exporter (`bf2export <mode> <GameDir> <OutDir>`, `Driver.cs` line 93: `GameDir` is any folder FrostySdk's `FileSystem` accepts) is then run with `GameDir` = that `ModData\<Profile>` folder. Its outputs are diffed against the vanilla export's manifests (`web/models.jsonl`, `textures.jsonl`, `anims.jsonl`, `data.tsv`) so only what a mod added or changed is kept, under `web_mods/<mod>/`. From there a mod hero goes through `scripts/bf2017-import.mjs` exactly as the game's own (MeshSets on the humanoid skeleton; their own rigs through `ownRig.js`) and its kit through `scripts/bf2017-abilities.mjs` and `bf2017-data.mjs`.

## Decisions

1. **Landing is Battlefront's front end.** On every landable world the title card is followed by a **mode menu**, the game's own cards: Galactic Assault, Starfighter Assault, Heroes vs Villains, Blast, the world's Story missions, Free roam. A mode a world lacks is shown dimmed with why (the game's level has no such layer; the site's map is not made yet; the war has no battle here now). The menu is data: `src/data/bf2017/modes.json` (the extractor's new `modes` command over the levels' layers, lane F) joined with the site's own availability (`missions/index.js`, `assaults.js`, the war, and the new game's routes as they land). Route: `/galaxy/:system/surface?mode=<id>`; `?mission=<id>` stays as the alias it is; `mode=free` is roam; the menu reopens from the surface's Menu ("Change mode") and the briefing page lists the same cards.
2. **Deploy before you step out.** The Loadout panel becomes the deploy screen: side (the world's era gives the pair: Rebels/Empire, Republic/Separatists, Resistance refused), then who (the heroes by side and the 2017 trooper classes from `classes.json` as playable kinds, each with the game's icon and string), outfit, weapon and mods, perks. It opens from the mode menu and from the Loadout button as before. **Equip is honest**: a 2017 body the page cannot fetch falls back to the site's committed figure for that hero (Luke, Han, Leia, Vader, Palpatine have one; the others fall back to the light cut, and failing that the humanoid stand-in on the game's rig) and says so once in the toast; the pick is never refused. The same fallback serves the party's lead on landing.
3. **The modes run on what exists first; the new game takes over as it lands.** Galactic Assault → `missions/assault.js` on its four maps today, and `/battlefront/<level>/galacticAssault` when the game design's lane 5 merges (the card's `to` is a table row, flipped in one place). Starfighter Assault → lane A. Heroes vs Villains and Blast → lane H on the surface engine. Story → the world's chases and quests. Free roam → the surface as now.
4. **Starfighter Assault from the four space levels.** `sb_endor_01`, `sb_fondor_01`, `sb_kamino_01` and `sb_droidbattleship_01` as level packs (`bf2017-level.mjs --frame map --no-fit --no-terrain`), the `SpaceBattle` layer read by lane 0's map extractor (a new `MODE_LAYERS` entry: spawns, the objective volumes and `PF_DestroyableObjective_*` prefabs, the intro and outro cameras), fought by `universe/battle.js`'s Fleet Assault sim with its phases bound to the level's objectives, the fighters and capital ships the export's own models (the site already carries the game's ARC-170 and A-wing; the rest come through `bf2017-import.mjs`). You reach it from the menu: the surface takes off and the galaxy page drops you into the battle at that system (`/galaxy/:system?battle=starfighter`), the war's own battle standing aside while it runs. Endor's is the first; it is the only one whose planet the site has (Fondor, Kamino's orbit and Ryloth come when their systems exist; Kamino's surface exists, so its orbit is second).
5. **Heroes vs Villains and Blast on the surface engine.** HvV: four against four from the thirteen heroes by side (light against dark; the mod heroes join as they land), in the level's `HeroArena` volumes (lane 0's rulebook has them for Hoth; the extractor runs for Endor, Tatooine, Geonosis, Kashyyyk), one **target** per team (the game's rule: a target's death scores, 10 to win; `source: "hand"`), saber bots on `duellists.js`'s mind and blaster bots on `hostiles.js`, the hero HUD. Blast: ten a side on the `TeamDeathmatch` layer's spawns, `assault.js`'s soldiers with no posts and a kill counter to 100, the game's score bar. Both as mission kinds in `missions/`, pure and seeded, with the arena test the assault has.
6. **The mods, through the desktop pipeline, as game data.** Lane D2 (a local session on the owner's machine: Frosty is a Windows GUI and the archives are there) applies one profile at a time and exports the difference as the section above says, then imports in this order: Battlefront Expanded's heroes and reinforcements the site lacks (Ahsoka on the game's rig replaces the Meshy one; Rex, Cody, Cad Bane, Mace Windu, Qui-Gon, Jango, Din Djarin, Thrawn, Ventress, Savage, Revan …), Saberfront's saber heroes, the clones' 4K and film looks (`clone`, `clonephase1` get them as outfits), Realistic Overhaul's weapon table as a second `weapons.json` variant the deploy screen can switch to, the HvV modes and Instant Action Overhaul V2 (when on the PC) read for their logic graphs and written up for lanes 2, 6 and H. Each imported model is credited `bf2017-mod-<slug>` with the mod's name, author and Nexus URL beside the game's permission line; nothing from a mod is published to the bucket before its credit row exists. **Sequel-era mod content is refused as the game's is** (Phasma, Finn, Kylo, Rey, Snoke, the First Order packs).
7. **The game's front-end look, on the site's kit.** The cards, the deploy screen and the mode titles use the game's own strings (`strings.json`; the extractor adds the mode and level names, `ID_GAMEMODE_*`, `ID_LEVEL_*`), its 702 icons and its two faces, on the runtime's HUD kit under the house rules (readable sizes, glass under text, one Menu, Esc never leaves a world). The frontend level (`Levels/Frontend/Frontend`, 582 instances) is noted and not used: the menu sits over the world you landed on, as the game's deploy screen does.
8. **The landing never holds without saying why.** The surface's prepare reports a step and a file; after 10 s on one step the veil names it; after 20 s it offers "Go in anyway" (the world as far as it got, the missing figure stood in by the fallback of decision 2); every asset fetch in the prepare path has a timeout (`assetBase.js`'s `WAIT_MS` is the model) and a 404 is a fallback, never a wait. The check (`scripts/landing-check.mjs`, extended) asserts the Hoth landing finishes under 25 s at high in a fronted headless tab with the bucket base and under 30 s with it absent.
9. **Nothing else moves.** No sequel era; the game design's "no budget rows under `src/components/battlefront/`" holds and the galaxy's budgets hold for the menu, the deploy screen and the two new missions (they are galaxy code); files under 800 lines; `src/lib` imports no React and no world; the universe and the other worlds unchanged.

## Departures
