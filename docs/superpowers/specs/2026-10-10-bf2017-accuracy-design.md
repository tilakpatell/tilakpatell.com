## 1. What the survey found

### 1.1 The colour chain is three records, and the site reads one

The game decides what a surface looks like in three places:

1. **`MeshVariationDatabase`** (3,610 records; 261 under `Levels/MP/`, one per level bundle: Hoth has `Hoth_01/`, `content/`, `fantasybattle/`, `heroarena/`). Each entry is a mesh, its materials, and **per material the texture bindings by parameter name** (`Color`, `_CS`, `_RGB`, `NS`, `F90ColorTexture`, `_NAM_texcoord0`, …) and a `MaterialVariation`; 17 of Hoth's 78 entries carry a `VariationAssetNameHash`, the snow-covered crates among them (`Box_M_01_A_Snow` binds `T_Box_M_01_A_M` and `_CS`). This is the authoritative colour-to-texture map; `materials.jsonl` (the desktop matdump lane Q1 reads) is the mesh's default material and agrees with the un-varied entries.
2. **`ObjectVariation`** (2,807 records, 2,344 of them `*_ShaderParameterVariation`): `MeshMaterialVariation` rows with plain-named `VectorParameters` (`PaintColour`, `MetalColour`, `GrungeColour_01`, `DetailTiling`, `ScorchEmberIntensity` 1600, …), `TextureParameters` and `ConditionalParameters` (`ESB_VehicleIsWreck` True, `ESB_PanelEnable` False). A variation is a tint and a switch set over a shader preset (`Shaders/Presets/Walrus/SS_VehicleLargePreset_01`).
3. **The shader depots** (`maps_work/shaderdepots.jsonl`, 36,188 rows, 726,690 parameter blocks, from `bf2export sbdprobe`): each compiled material's live parameter blocks with hashed names. **The hash is djb2-xor of the lower-cased name** (`h = ((h << 5) + h) ^ c` from 5381): a dictionary of the 1,812 parameter names in `materials.jsonl` resolves 172,539 of the parameters; the type hashes `Vec` (`0b87fa95`) and `Boolean` (`0d1cfa1b`) resolve the same way; nine names appear in every block (216,497 each: the engine's per-block constants) and 2,411 names are unresolved, most of them rare. This is what the vehicles check (#866) called "shader-graph parameters not in the export": they are, hashed, and the dictionary names them.

What the site reads today: lane Q1's `recipes.json` from `materials.jsonl` (the default material), lane Q4's weathering gates, and the vehicles' `--textures` hand binding from the depot probe. **It reads no variation**: every crate on Hoth is the un-snowed one, every Yavin bush the light one (68 `YavinBase_Bush_Medium_02_Skinned_Dark` references in the level records), every Naboo hangar crate dry (39 `CrateHangar_02_Rain`), every lit Yavin terminal unlit (27 `ComputerTerminal_01_A_Lit`). Across the multiplayer levels 515 instance groups name a variation outright, and the per-instance hashes of the static model groups (`InstanceObjectVariation`, empty in the dump like `InstanceTransforms` were) live in the same Havok resource the map transforms came from.

### 1.2 The bots' minds: lane 1 took the tactics; the system, the cover scores, the difficulties, the names and the creatures are unread

`src/data/bf2017/ai.json` holds 10 templates, 16 tactics, 20 AI weapons, 202 firing patterns, 3 cover rows, 14 difficulty rows and 23 instant-action rows, and `soldierBrain.js` plays the tactics' engage, suppression, close-combat and cover-request branches. Unread in the export:

- **`AI/BattleAI/System/AISystem`** (and `AISystem_PvE`): the targeting scores (`TargetDistanceEvaluation`: front 0.5, back 0.7, human preference 2.5, low-health scale, elevation scales, `VisibleTargetLimit`), `SquadEngageData` (engage 7.5 s, find cover 4 s, cooldown 4 s, secondary target 12 m, cover-fire target 40 m), area booking radii (2.5 m), `Shooting` (keep firing at a player 4 s, at an AI 2 s), alertness and flee-from-grenade flags, the balance distribution and the target coordinator (start 10, ideal 0.5, end 30).
- **`CoverConstants`, `CoverConstants_PvE`, the two `CoverScoreAsset`s and 41 `CoverQueryData`** (attack, hide, flee, lost-target, protective, sentry, charge): the queries the brain's `pickCover` approximates by hand.
- **The Skirmish tactics and templates** (`Assault/Heavy/Officer/Specialist_PvE_Tactics`, their Separatist twins, the `*_Skirmish_Template`s, `AI_*_PvE` weapons and the `*_Ability_PvE` rows): the game's bots for Instant Action and Arcade, where the bots use abilities.
- **`GameDifficultySettings`** (15 difficulties with float curves: damage, aim, reaction) read only in part.
- **`AIPlayerNames`** (50 records: `TK-772`, `TK-350` … for the Empire in Starfighter Assault; per faction per mode) where the site's bots are unnamed.
- **The living world**: 139 `Gameplay/Characters/LivingWorld/Prefab` blueprints (`LV_Endor_Civilians_2`: `CreatureFollowWaypointsEntityData`), 33 `CreatureLocoSettings` with world-event reactions (`ExternalInfluence_HumanPlayer`: consideration range 4 m, probability 1, alignment rate 0.1), `CreatureLocoBindings`; the 3,425 placed actors lane E0 lists in `actors.json` have these minds.
- **The vehicles' AI** (`ATAT_AI`, `ATST_AI` weapon rows; `AI/BattleAI/Vehicles`) and the heroes: no hero-bot record exists (the heroes' decisions are logic graphs), so lane H's hand rules stand and are marked.

### 1.3 The screens: the site draws 14 HUD parts from 84 widgets; the game has 1,082

`UI/Frontend/Screens/` holds the front end as widget blueprints (Start, MainMenu, Play, PlayMultiplayer, PlayMultiplayerHvsV, PlaySkirmish and its five Skirmish screens, PlayCampaign, MissionSelect, Customization, Weapons, Options, the pairing and EULA flow; 60 screens, 263 frontend widgets, 122 prefabs), each a tree of elements with `Anchor`, `Size`, `Offset`, `Position`, `Color`, `Alpha` on the 1920 × 1080 reference and links (`GotoUILinkTargetEntityData`) to the next screen; `UI/Data/GameModes/` (61 information assets: 16 modes, 29 levels, 16 planets, with their names, descriptions and tiles' keys); 48 `WSUILoadingScreenAsset` with 80 hints; `UI/InGame/Spawn` (87), `InGameMenu` (37), `EndOfRound` (96), the kill screen's 36 SVGs; and **302 `DiceUIVectorShapeAsset`** (136 of the HUD: crosshairs per hero and weapon, 29 in-world markers, 20 game-mode, 13 end-of-round) as vector paths with colours, corner radii and triangulated points, exactly what the game rasterises. `UIElementFontStyle` (55) names each text's face and size; `WSUICppScreenData` (18) and the state groups name the screens' states. Lane 0 flattened 84 HUD widgets (`ui.json`), lane M cut the films, fonts, icons and strings, lane 5 drew 14 HUD parts, lane F drew a mode menu and a deploy panel of its own design. None draws a front-end screen, a vector shape, a loading screen, the in-game menu or the end-of-round sequence as the game lays them out.

### 1.4 Starfighter Assault: the game's fighters, their handling and their squadron minds are all in the data

Lane A fought SB_Endor_01 and SB_Kamino_01 on the galaxy's own ships with the galaxy's flight model. The export has, for each of 43 air vehicles under `Gameplay/Vehicles/Air/` (`XWing_T65`, `Xwing_Red5`, `AWing`, `BTLYwing`, `Ywing`, `TieFighter`, `TieInterceptor`, `TieBomber`, `TieAdvancedX1`, `MillenniumFalcon`, `Slave1`, `ARC170`, `VWing`, `N1Starfighter`, `VultureDroid`, `DroidTriFighter`, `HyenaBomber`, `YodasStarfighter`, `Scimitar`, `LAAT`, `Uwing` …; the sequel's refused): a `_Handling` layer with the numbers (`MaxSpeed` 100, `BoostMaxSpeed` 115, `EngineAccelerationRate` 32.5, `BoostEngineAccelerationRate` 60, `RollTorque` -31,500, `TurnRateBySpeedCurve`, `AxisTurnRates` and the zoomed set, `TargetFakeRollAngle` 32°, the self-right factors), so the physics design's note that the handling layers hold no speeds was about the ground vehicles and is wrong for the starfighters; `_Weapons`, `_Targeting`, `_Camera`, `_Destruction`, `_FX`, `_Abilities` layers; the blueprint's `StarfighterPhysicsComponentData`, `StarfighterHealthComponentData`, `WeaponFiringData` and the cockpit `StaticCameraData`; and 451 records under `Gameplay/Vehicles/Abilities/Starfighters/` (108 passive, 20 basic, 10 retriggerable, 8 vehicle-weapon abilities, 18 weapon unlocks and firing tables). **The squadron AI is 29 `SquadronAIBehaviourBlueprint`s of typed behaviour-tree nodes** (`Selector`, `Sequence`, `Loop`, `Random`, `Timed`; `DogfightingAttack`, `DogfightingEvade`, `DogfightingFlyForward`, `SideToSide`, `Turn`, `FollowWaypoints`, `EscapeAreas`, `ProximateAreas`; the `CannonWeaponRule` and `MissileWeaponRule` with `MinDistanceForAttack` 100, `MaxDistanceForAttack` 500, `MaxTargeterAngle` 75°, `LockOnDelayTime` 3 s, `MinTimeBetweenContinuousFire` 1.5 s, `MaxContinuousFireTime` 2 s, `ProjectileSpeed` 2,000), with `AIChannelAsset`s binding them. The space mode's own records (`PF_SpaceBattles_PhaseGameplayLogic_V03`, the objective prefabs, `PF_KitLimitations_SpaceBattles`, the marker colour and text logic, five `AINames_*_SpaceBattles`) and the HUD's `HUD_ObjectivesScreen_SpaceBattles_V05` widget (already in `ui.json`), 42 `UI/SVG/SPaceBattles` icons and 35 in-world marker records complete it. Fondor and the droid battleship wait on nothing but a pack and an area of their own, as Kamino was given.

### 1.5 The maps: 44 usable, three packs on `main`, the factory running

`main` has Hoth_01 (lane L), SB_Endor_01 and SB_Kamino_01 (lane A) as packs; lane E0 (running, `claude/bf2017-e0-factory`) is the factory for the rest and E1 to E5 are planned per world and wait on it. Rulebooks exist for six ground maps (Hoth, Endor, Tatooine, Geonosis, Kashyyyk and the arenas) and four space maps; `modes.json` names 27 levels on 15 worlds. What no lane measures is **a map's accuracy**: which of its sub-levels are drawn, how many of its instances, lights, decals, effects, actors and vehicle spawns reach the screen, whether its variations are applied, whether its modes' spawns and objectives are its own. The game design's lane 6 (the other modes and the other maps' rulebooks) is unstarted and its blockers (lanes 2 and 5) merged today.

### 1.6 The picture: seven lanes merged, three unstarted, and the game's world not yet on the whole stack

Merged: fidelity S (PCSS sun, cloud shadows, the far cache), V (volumetrics, media fog, god rays, flares), X (the emitters' particles), C (the cameras); surfaces Q1 (the game material and recipes), Q2 (the ground's layers), Q4 (weathering and decals); engine R and T (the node renderer and the stack); the laptop runs (#863, #872: the ultra chain at 4.8 ms on an RTX 5090, SSGI composited). ## 2. The approaches weighed

1. **One accuracy lane that does all six.** Rejected: six different data families, six different consumers; one session would take days and merge against six moving fronts.
2. **Hand the gaps to the running lanes.** Rejected: E0 is the factory and must not grow a material decoder; lane 5 (running) is the game world's follow-ups; M is the fonts' PR; none has room for the screens or the squadron minds. The seams with them are stated instead (§5).
3. **Six lanes by data family, each with a measure, plus the three unstarted picture plans spawned as they stand** (chosen). Colour (the variation chain), bots (the minds), screens (the widget trees and shapes), fighters (Starfighter Assault from the vehicles' records), maps (the accuracy ledger and the other maps' rulebooks and modes), picture (the stack under the game's world and the proof on a GPU); Q6, N and U from their own plans. Each lane's gate is a number in its hand-off row.

## 3. The design, by lane

### Lane colour: the variation chain

**Files**: `scripts/bf2017-variations.mjs` and `scripts/lib/bf2017-variations.mjs` (pure, tested on a fixture of one level's `MeshVariationDb_Win32.json` and two `ObjectVariation` records); `scripts/lib/bf2017-shader-names.mjs` (the djb2-xor dictionary: `hashName(name)`, `resolveDepot(rows, names)`; the names from `materials.jsonl`'s keys, the `ObjectVariation` records' `ParameterName`s and the `SurfaceShaderPreset` records' parameter lists); `src/data/bf2017/shaderParams.json` (name → hash, and the unresolved hashes by count); `variations.json` beside each level pack (`{ format: 1, meshes: { <mesh>: { default: { <material>: { textures: { slot: path }, vectors, bools } }, variations: { <name>: { … } } } }, instances: [[groupIndex, variationName]] | null }`); `src/components/galaxy/surface/level/levelVariations.js` (reads `variations.json`, hands a material's texture overrides and vectors to Q1's `materialFor` through `levelGltf.js`'s recipe path: a recipe gains `variation`); `scripts/bf2017-material-audit.mjs` (every drawn material of a pack against the MVDB's bindings: `bound`, `default-only`, `missing-texture`, `no-entry`; a table per world under `docs/superpowers/evidence/bf2017-colour/`).

**The chain.** For a level: read its bundle's MVDBs (the level's own and its sub-levels'), key each entry by mesh and `VariationAssetNameHash` (0 = default), resolve each `ObjectVariation` the entries name (its vectors, bools, conditionals, textures), and write `variations.json`. For the per-instance link: the static model groups' `InstanceObjectVariation` lists are empty in the dump as the transforms were, so the instance hashes come from the same Havok resource `bf2export physgroups` read (`maps_work/smg_transforms.jsonl` gains a `variation` column; the desktop step is one task, run by the owner or a local session, and the cloud lane proceeds without it); until it lands, **the level's own rule**: a mesh whose level MVDB carries exactly one variation and whose default entry no sub-level of that level names takes that variation everywhere on that level (Hoth's snowed crates; Yavin's dark bushes where the level names only the dark one), and the audit counts the rows decided by the rule apart.

**The shader-graph gap closed properly.** The vehicles whose materials name a graph (#866's `--textures` hand table: `SS_ATAT_Head`, the Falcon's details and legs) take their bindings from the MVDB entries of their bundles (`Characters/Hero/*/vur_*_bpb/`, `Gameplay/Vehicles/*/…`) through the same writer: `bf2017-import.mjs --variations <bundle>` binds by name, and the hand table is retired when the audit shows the same maps.

**The characters' variations** (the troopers' markings, the heroes' outfits' palettes: `ObjectVariation` under `Characters/`) are in scope only as data (`variations.json` rows for the imported kinds); applying them to the crew is cast-left T's atlas work and the lane says so in the hand-off.

**Gate.** `node scripts/bf2017-material-audit.mjs hoth` ≥ 95 % of Hoth's drawn materials `bound`; `sb_endor`, `sb_kamino` and the first E pack on `main` reported; `shaderParams.json` resolves ≥ 90 % of the depot parameters by count; the Hoth crate stands snowed in a shot beside the game's (the fixture page `scripts/surfaces-check.mjs` gains `--variations`).
