## 1. What the survey found that the hand-off does not say

### 1.1 The "waiting on uploads" are not waiting: they were never queued

`web_optimize.py` (the desktop's web build) chooses the textures to encode by reading each **packed model's LOD0 GLB** and listing the images it binds (`glb_images(packed)`, lines 129 to 149). A texture a model reaches any other way is never listed:

- a **markings or palette map** bound by an `ObjectVariation` → `MeshMaterialVariation` (the clone legions' `Characters/Markings/501/L_Assault_Preq_01_CM_T_501`: `Markings_Color`, `Markings_Color2`, `Markings_ChannelSelection`, `Markings_Use2Masks`; the civilians' `Civ_MosEisley_01_V01`: `ColorClothes` 0.4 and `ColorHairSkin` 0.6, a row of the kind's `_p` palette; the B1's `SS_CharactersPreset_RobotMarkings`), 578 of the 763 textures the 5,254 character variation records name are not in the bucket;
- a **shader parameter** map (the eye shader's `T_Eye_MP_DA`, `_A`, `_AO`, `_N`);
- an **LOD cap** (`T_LODCaps_Braids_01_*`, `t_lodcap_bob_02_*`, `t_lodcaps_ewok_*`), bound by a lower LOD's material only;
- an **effect sheet** bound by an effect graph, not a mesh (159 of the 173 `FX/Textures/**` maps in the manifest are not up);
- anything the model curation left out (UI bitmaps, `objects/nature`, most of `levels/sp`).

In all: **11,101 of the 17,511 textures in `textures.jsonl` have no row in `upload_state.tsv`, and none of them is encoded on disk.** The encoder was not the cause: its last pass (`logs/pipeline.log`, 23:44) read `10800/11802 ok=10684 skipped=115 failed=1`, exited 1 for that one failed file, and the pipeline relaunched it five times before giving up ("encode gave up after repeated crashes" in `pipeline_status.txt`). The upload passes finished with `failed 0`. Every name the hand-off lists as "waiting" is in the 11,101: `t_lodcaps_braids_01_brown_cm`, `t_eye_mp_da`, every `characters/markings/**` map (72), `t_civ_moseisley_01_p`, `t_civ_theed_01_p`, `t_ewok_01_hood_*`, `T_BlasterProjectileSide_02_D`, `MuzzleBlast`, `T_XWingEngineLight_02_D`.

So the first lane is a desktop pass with a **list**, the way the planet skins went (`ktx2_encode.py --list`, `upload_supabase.py --root`): the list is computed from the data, not typed.

### 1.2 The audio exists as records and chunks; the exporter has no sound pass

`data/Sound/` holds 3,231 `SoundPatchAsset` records (`Loudness`, `Radius`, `IsLooping`, `DopplerFactor`, a graph, and `ReferencedData[].SoundData` naming a `SoundWaveAsset`). They name **4,588 distinct `SoundWaveAsset`s**: 377 footstep, 316 blaster, 178 TIE, 125 voice-over, 97 Yoda, 45 lightsaber, 44 AT-AT, 31 tauntaun, 30 Ewok, 28 Vader, 18 snowspeeder. The `SoundWaveAsset` records themselves were skipped by `EbxDump.cs` as media ("the mesh, texture and audio passes cover" them, line 23) and no audio pass was ever written: `tool/*.cs` has no sound code. The Frosty install's `Plugins/SoundEditorPlugin.dll` is Frosty's own sound plugin (it reads a `SoundWaveAsset`'s chunks and segments and decodes EA's codecs to PCM; it is how Frosty Editor's "Export" on a sound works). `bf2export` already loads `MeshSetPlugin.dll` by reference and `ShaderDepotProbe.cs` reads a plugin's types by reflection, so a `SoundExport.cs` that does the same for sounds is a known pattern, not a port. (A PowerShell reflection listing of the plugin's types overflowed the stack; the lane lists them from C#.)

## 3. The design, by lane

### Lane D: the desktop. The textures that were never queued, and the sound export

**Owner's machine only** (`Downloads\BF2_Extract`, not the repo; the owner's permission covers it). Two passes.

**D1, the texture gap.** `tool/wanted_textures.py` computes the list the build never made: every `TextureAsset` named by (a) an `ObjectVariation`/`MeshMaterialVariation`/`ShaderParameterVariation` record under `data/Characters/**` and `data/Gameplay/**` whose mesh is in `web_opt/models.jsonl` (the packed set); (b) `data/Shaders/Presets/*` reached from those meshes' materials; (c) the LOD caps and shared head maps under `Characters/Heads/Texture_Shared/**` and `Characters/Heads/_Shared/**`; (d) every `FX/Textures/**` and `Addons/*/FX/**` map (the 173, and lane F's `WANTED` by name); minus what `upload_state.tsv` has. Output `web_opt/_wanted_list.tsv` (`png<TAB>class`, the class by `textures.jsonl`'s `srgb` and the name's suffix as `web_optimize.py` does, with the sRGB override `tool/ktx2_colorset.py` applies). Then `tool/ktx2_encode.py --list web_opt/_wanted_list.tsv --max 2048` (hybrid: ETC1S colour, UASTC the rest, as the models' maps were), hard-link the raw PNGs beside them as the planet skins were, `queue()` both lists, and send with `upload_supabase.py` on a de-duplicated list (the 2026-10-10 00:40 way), big files through `upload_tus.py`. Expected: about 1,400 maps (578 variation + 159 fx + the caps and eyes), under 2 GB. The one encoder failure is found by `grep FAILED logs/pipeline.log` and re-queued or named in the hand-off.

**D2, the sound export.** `tool/SoundExport.cs` in `bf2export`: references `Plugins/SoundEditorPlugin.dll` (as `MeshSetPlugin.dll` is referenced in the csproj) and, for each `SoundWaveAsset` named by a `SoundPatchAsset` under `data/Sound/**` (the 4,588; `--filter` narrows by path, `--list` takes names), reads its `Chunks`/`Segments`/`RuntimeVariations`, decodes each variation with the plugin's decoder to 16-bit PCM WAV at the chunk's rate and channels, and writes `web/audio/<asset path>/v<N>.wav` plus a `web/audio.jsonl` row `{ name, file, variation, seconds, rate, channels, codec, loop, patch: [the patches that use it] }`. Then `tool/audio_encode.py`: ffmpeg to Opus 64 kbit/s mono (`.opus`) and MP3 96 kbit/s for Safari (`.mp3`), queued and uploaded under `web/audio/`. Priority order (so the site can wire while the rest runs): the 61 names in `gameSounds.js` first (the lane resolves each to a patch: `Sound/Weapons/LightSaber/**` ignite, swing, clash, hum; `Sound/Weapons/Blasters/**` by weapon; `Sound/Characters/Movement/**` footsteps by surface; `Sound/Vehicles/**` engines; `Sound/VO/**` the lines by hero and situation), then everything the beasts, walkers and vehicles the site places name, then the rest. The patch records' `Loudness`, `Radius` and `IsLooping` go into the jsonl: they are the site's gain and falloff.

Nothing of lane D is tested in the repo; its gate is `node scripts/bf2017-fx.mjs --count` (the fx sheets: from 55 to 173), `node scripts/bf2017-audio.mjs` (from 0 audio files to the count), and the re-imports named in §5.
