# The lightsaber as the 2017 game's: one path, the game's hit rules, hitboxes, block, stamina, dash. The design

Date: 2026-10-10 (night). Status: **design, for one Opus 5.5 implementation session (lane S).** Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-10-bf2017-saber-laneS.md`. It stands on lane X's stroke tables (`HANDOFF-bf2017.md`, "Lane X, the sabers"), phase 1's heroes on the game's rig, lane P2's bone capsules, and replaces the site's own saber rules (`combatRules.js`'s stances, guard, parry, dodge) with the game's, read from its records.

## What the owner asked

"There's an issue where custom assets like Ahsoka are interfering with lightsaber mechanics. Make the lightsaber mechanics exactly like they are in Battlefront 2: look at the code and replicate everything, including the hitboxes and everything, then implement with Opus 5.5."

## What was found (2026-10-10, the export at `C:\Users\tilak\Downloads\BF2_Extract\web`, index `data.tsv`; the same records are in the bucket's `data/`)

### Two saber paths on the site, and Ahsoka on the wrong one

`surface/saber.js` branches on `fig.rig === 'walrus'` (line 137): a 2017 hero plays the game's strokes from its table (`gameStance.js`, `stanceFromTable.js`, lane X) with the hilt in `Wep_Root`; **anyone else** plays UAL2's sword clips from the site's `STANCES` table, poses its arms by hand, holds a second hilt in the left hand for `dual`, and is hit on Meshy's ten hurtbox regions instead of the game's capsules. The only saber hero on that second path is **Ahsoka** (`heroes.js` line 78: a Meshy figure, `stance: 'dual'`, no `rig`). Her clips, timings, reach, second hilt and hurtbox are the site's, not the game's, and the site's guard, parry and dodge numbers (`GUARD`, `PARRY`, `DODGE` in `combatRules.js`, "drawn from how the films' games do it") apply to every hero, 2017 or not. That is the interference: two rule sets in one fight.

## Decisions

1. **One saber path: the game's rig.** `saber.js` loses its non-walrus branch (the UAL sword clips, the hand-posed arms, the second hilt under `LeftHand`, `stance: 'dual'`, `STANCES`/`HEAVY`/`DIRS`/`BLOCK_CLIP` from `combatRules.js`). A saber hero is a `rig: 'walrus'` figure with a stroke table; nothing else fences. **Ahsoka** leaves the saber roster until lane D2 brings her from Battlefront Expanded on the game's rig (her row keeps her name and lines with `soon: 'on the game's rig'`; the deploy screen shows her dimmed with that reason; her hilt row stays for then). The Death Star interior's own saber (`deathstar/inside/scene/saber.js`) is another world and is not touched.
2. **The rules are the game's, as data.** `scripts/bf2017-data.mjs saber` (pure part `scripts/lib/bf2017-rulebook-saber.mjs`, fixtures of the cut records) writes `src/data/bf2017/saber.json`: per hero the damage rows (initial, delay, from behind, the variants), the hit query (the sphere radii, each cone's distance and angle and what it feeds, from the prefab's graph), the deflect (the shield's radius and offset, the stamina drain and refill, the deflected projectile), the stagger durations, the dash (cost, activation, active, recharge, charges), the throw (Vader, Maul), the hero physics (sprint, backward, gains), the health and regen, the camera floats, the AI melee projectile, and the state channels and requests by name. Every number carries its `_source`. `src/lib/combat/saber2017.js` is the pure engine over it: `createSaberSim(rules, hero)` with `strike`, `block`, `dash`, `step`, `hitsFor` and `events`; tested in Node against the rulebook; used by you and by every duellist.
3. **The hit is the game's query, the blade is where it lands.** Inside a strike's contact window (the stroke table's), the attacker's targets are the records' queries: the 8.5 m sphere narrowed by the cones (1.5 m / 35°, 1.8 m / 60°, the 45° and 40° gates) in the attacker's facing, shielded targets out, each target hit once a strike; the damage applies `DelayInitialDamageTime` after the window opens (Luke 0.09 s), `FromBehind` added when the attacker is behind the target's facing (the records' rule for "behind": the target's forward against the attacker's position, the angle lane S reads from the `FromBehind` affector's conditions, else 90°, `hand`). The point of contact for FX and the head/chest/limb region comes from `blade.js`'s sweep against the target's capsules (`defaultsoldierbonecollision_hero` for heroes, the troopers' sets, the game's own), never from a different decision: if the query says hit and the sweep found no point, the nearest capsule point is used. Hitboxes are therefore the game's capsule sets on the game's skeleton for every saber hero and every 2017 kind; a Meshy figure keeps `hurtbox.js` only as a target of blaster bolts, never of a saber (there are none in a fight after decision 1).
4. **The block is the shield sphere, with stamina.** Holding block raises the hero's deflect: a 1.2 m sphere 0.45 m ahead of the hero's position, facing-bound by the deflect's angle filters (read by lane S; the game blocks only what comes from the front). A bolt whose flown segment enters the sphere while deflecting is turned (`BlasterProjectile_SaberDeflect_<colour>` back along the deflect transform's aim, auto-aimed at the shooter as `PF_DeflectTransform_AutoAim` says) and costs stamina; a saber strike whose query reaches a deflecting target inside its front arc is blocked: the attacker takes `BlockedLightSaber` (0.5 s), the defender's stamina drops by the strike's cost; a strike from behind is not blocked and takes the from-behind damage. Stamina refills after its pause; at zero the block drops and the defender staggers for the records' time. `GUARD`, `PARRY`, `blockOutcome` and the parry window go; the game has no parry.
5. **The dash is the game's dodge.** Two charges on a bar (`TriggerCost 0.5`), 0.8 s activation, 1.0 s active, 10 s recharge, input-queued; the distance and the i-frames are the dash clip's (`strokes/<hero>.json`'s `dash` and `dodges` rows, `Affector_Evading` for the window, `Affector_Evading_DMGMultiplier` if it says damage is reduced rather than nil). `DODGE` goes.
6. **The lunge and the turn are the clip's root motion plus the game's target snap.** A strike plays its clip's root rows unscaled (the game moves the hero by the animation); the turn toward the target is the aim-assist's: the target picked by the hit query's priorities (the `AimAssistCollisionBoneData` priorities and radius scales of the hero bone set), within the query's cone. `STRIKE`, `lunge`, `rootScale` and the 0.25 rad arm turn go.
7. **Bots fence by the same engine.** `duellists.js` drives `saber2017.js` for each duellist; `duel.js`'s mind keeps its states but its timings are the stroke table's and its block decision is the game's deflect (a bot holds block when a strike's query would reach it and it has stamina; it attacks when yours is spent), and the AI's own melee is `AIMeleeProjectile`'s numbers when a bot strikes a trooper. `DUEL`'s hand numbers go where the records give one.
8. **Clash.** Two strikes whose queries reach each other in the same step: both take `BlockedLightSaber`, neither lands (the game's clash), the FX at the blades' crossing (`blade.js`'s `clash`).
9. **The camera, the HUD.** A saber hero's third-person camera takes the `Hero_Lightsaber_*_Camera_3P` floats through the galaxy's camera (lane C's `applyGameCamera` if merged, else the surface's camera numbers with a `_source`); the HUD shows the stamina bar and the dash charges (the game's `PF_Hud_Ability_Stamina` widget as a kit part), the combat numbers event gains `stamina` and `dashes`.
10. **Nothing else moves.** The Force abilities (`abilityRules.js`, `powers.js`) stay as they are (the fourth design's lane); blasters and bolts (`bolt.js`, `boltPlay.js`) change only where the shield sphere replaces the raised-blade `guard` test; the universe's online protocol keeps `STANCE_IDS` exported (an empty-compatible list: `['single', 'double']`, the game's) so the flight pages still build; files under 800 lines; the galaxy's budgets hold; no sequel heroes (Rey, Kylo refused by the extractor as ever).

## Open assumptions, marked

1. The stamina's numbers are in the deflect prefab's synced floats or the hero's gameplay asset; if neither names them plainly, the rulebook carries the game's observed values as `hand` and the hand-off says which.
2. Channel value 17 on the damage affectors is the attacking action state; `AllRequests` decodes it.

## Departures

One line each, added by the lane when it merges.

- **The hit query’s shape** (decision 3): the cones hang off an anchor 1.5 m *behind* the striker (a `TransformMultiplier`, trans.z −1.5): a target is struck within the 3 m `SphereQuery` (Controllables) round that point, outside 1.5 m of it and within 35° of the facing from it, inside the 45° gate looking back from a point 5 m ahead of it; that reaches 0–1.5 m ahead of the striker and, at a metre, one beside it. The 8.5 m sphere (a `ConditionalFloat` sets it to 8, or 2 out of stamina), the 1.8 m / 60° filter and the 40° pick (distance × 0.2, angle × 2, one result) are the *lunge’s* target query, written to the animation; they decide what a strike turns to, not what it hits. “Behind” is the target’s facing within 70° of the striker’s (`MaxTargetAngleFromForwardDirection`), not 90° by hand.
- **Who takes `BlockedLightSaber`** (decision 4): `PF_BlockStaggerAffectors` applies it (0.5 s) to the query’s blocked set, the *defender*, whose deflect prefab drains its stamina on receiving it; the striker’s bounce (its `LightAttack_Blocked` clip) is timed by the same 0.5 s.
- **The dash** (decision 5) is the hero dodge, `Ability_Evade_CharacterState` (cost 0.5, two a bar, 0.4 s active, 2.2 s recharge; Dooku’s three at 0.33), not `Ability_Luke_Dash`, which is Luke’s Rush, a Force ability (`abilityRules.js`’s `rush`), left untouched. Its distance is the pack’s `dodge.<way>` clip’s root travel (Luke’s 3.3–3.5 m); while it’s on, damage is × 0.8 (`Affector_Evading_DMGMultiplier`), not nil.
- **The lunge** (decision 6): the game’s animation warps a strike’s root to the target the query hands it (the prefab writes its position to the Ant state, which isn’t exported); unscaled, Luke’s first strike lunges 2 m before its window opens and passes a close target. The site plays the root unscaled but stops it with the target in the middle of the query’s ring, 0.75 m short, from the query’s own numbers.
- **The block’s front** (decision 4) is the shield’s own shape, not an angle filter: the `HeroShield` blueprint’s part box (Vader’s and Grievous’s their own), a shell from just behind the hero to 0.85 m ahead, so a bolt from behind meets the body first. The stamina is all in the records (`Online/BattlepointCostData`’s `LIGHTSABER_COSTS_*`, scaled by the deflect prefab’s Math nodes): a strike of your own, a blocked strike, a bolt by its damage, the regen and its delay; only the break’s stagger (1.2 s) is `hand`.
- **Damage in the site’s units**: a strike on a target in the site’s hit points (`abilityRules.js`’s `GAME_HP`, 75: Luke’s 130 is 1.73, 160 from behind 2.13, unrounded); on you, as a share of your hero’s health (Vader’s 130 on Luke’s 750 is 17 of 100).
- **Not wired**: the camera floats (in `saber.json` by their Ant property; lane C’s `applyGameCamera` isn’t on `main`); the hero physics (movement, not the saber’s); the kick (strike index 7: the stroke tables hold no kick); the raycast fallback (vehicles only); `AIMeleeProjectile`’s 75 is what a brawler’s swipe costs your shield, its damage on you stays the spawn’s. `saberOf` lives in `lib/combat/saber2017.js`, not `lib/battlefront/rulebook.js`.
- **The clash** (decision 8) has no record of its own: the engine’s rule is two strikes whose queries reach each other in one window, both recoiling `BlockedLightSaber`’s time.
- **Off the rig**: the Inquisitor (a Meshy figure) holds a lit prop and swipes as a brawler; a clone or the Emperor on the game’s rig fences by Luke’s strokes and row (`hand.standIn`).
- **Heroes vs Villains** (lane H, merged first): its saber bots were wired to the engine here (decision 7’s “whichever lands second”): each its own sim, its strike’s lunge by its clip’s root, its block raised for a strike that would reach it and while it’s shot at (the hand 70 % turn gone); your saber reaches its fighters, its strikes on you decided by the engine.
