## Bringing in the remaster's models

The owner holds Harrisonfog's permission to use the Battlefront 2 Remaster's models here. They can't be fetched from a cloud session (ModDB turns automated fetches away, and the remaster is a 4.8 GB installer of munged `.lvl` files), so they come in from the owner's copy:

1. Get a model out of the game's files with the mod tools (the SWBF2 modtools' `.msh` and `.tga` sources, where the remaster's are to hand; or an `.lvl` unmunger, then Blender's SWBF msh add-on to export a `.glb`). Put the `.msh` and its `.tga` textures in one folder (or the textures in a `textures/` folder beside it).
2. `node scripts/battlefront-import.mjs <folder>/imp_inf_snowtrooper.msh --kind snowtrooper --as 'the snowtroopers' --metres 1.83`. It writes `public/models/galaxy/surface/snowtrooper.glb`, the catalogue line in `src/components/galaxy/surface/catalog/battlefront.js` and the credit (`surface-snowtrooper`, `license: 'permission'`) in `src/data/modelCredits.json`. A `.glb`, `.gltf` or `.fbx` goes in the same way (the `.fbx` through the dev server, `npx vite --port 5188`).
3. Look at it: `node scripts/glb-shot.mjs public/models/galaxy/surface/snowtrooper.glb /tmp/snowtrooper.png three,front,side` (the dev server up). Lying down: `--up z`. Facing away: `--yaw 3.1416`. Mirrored (its left hand holding the gun): `--mirror`. Textures upside down: `--keep-v`.
4. `npx vitest run src/components/galaxy/surface/catalog src/data` (the catalogue and credit tests cover the new kind), `npm run credits` for `CREDITS.md`'s section, then commit the `.glb`, the two source files and `CREDITS.md`.

The kinds the battles field, to import under those names so they take over at once: Hoth, `snowtrooper` (the Empire) and `hothtrooper` (the Rebellion; `rebel` is the plain Rebel soldier elsewhere); Geonosis, `clone`, `battledroid`, `superdroid`. Other worlds' troopers: `stormtrooper`, `scouttrooper`, `shoretrooper`, `deathtrooper`, `sandtrooper`. A new kind (say `darktrooper`) is usable in a map's `kinds` as soon as it's imported. The imported model is a still mesh walked with a bob (`actors.js`'s `modelFigure`); `--rig` keeps its skeleton for the posing item under Left, and `scripts/lib/msh.mjs` already reads the bones a skin names.

**Brought in, all ten (2026-10-06).** From the remaster’s current sides, Harrisonfog’s *Improved Sides 3* (`ISM3_2025_Latest.2.zip` on ModDB, the update to the remaster installer’s sides): `side/imp.lvl`, `all.lvl`, `rep.lvl` and `cisism.lvl`, each unmunged with [swbf-unmunge](https://github.com/PrismaticFlower/swbf-unmunge) (`swbf-unmunge -file imp.lvl` writes `imp/msh/*.msh` and `imp/textures/*.tga`, where the import looks). The files: `imp_inf_snowtrooper`, `all_inf_snowtrooper` (hothtrooper), `rep_inf_ep3trooper` (clone, the 501st), `cis_inf_bdroid`, `cis_inf_sbdroid`, `imp_inf_stormtrooper`, `imp_inf_scout`, `imp_inf_shoretrooper`, `imp_inf_deathtrooper`, `imp_inf_sandtrooper1`; none needed a flag (their left bones are at +x, facing +z). They stand in their bind pose, a T or an A (Left, 2). What the real files needed of the import: a skin comes as parts all named `override_texture`, and the armour’s alpha is its shine (material flag 128), so only flags 4, 16 and 64 make a texture see-through (`mshLook`). On Windows, point `CHROME` at Edge (`C:/Program Files (x86)/Microsoft/Edge/Application/msedge.exe`) for `glb-shot.mjs` and `assault-check.mjs`, and `BASE` at the dev server if it isn’t on 5188.

## Done since: Scarif, Endor, cover and waves (2026-10-07)

- **Two more maps** in `missions/assaults.js`: *The Battle of Scarif* (the Pathfinders, `rebel`, against shoretroopers and death troopers: the beach and the bunker line, Pad Nine, the master switch; Scarif's briefing is this battle now, `status: 'live'`, and the Rogue One flight is back on the games design's list) and *The Battle of Endor* (the strike team and Ewoks, `rebel` and `ewok`, against stormtroopers and scouts: the scouts' camp and the log trap, the bunker, the shield generator; Endor's `game.also` beside the chase). `index.test.js` covers them as it covers the first two.
- **Cover and suppression** in `assault.js` (pure, tested in `assault.test.js`), on top of the squads another lane shipped the same day (`lib/ai/squad`: squads by reach, their nerve and posture, the frontline, shot tokens): a bolt fired at a soldier raises its `suppress`, over `RULES.suppressed` it shoots at half accuracy and moves at a crouch; a soldier kept down (or under 40% hp) finds cover on the far side of the nearest solid from its shooter (`coverFrom`, from `env.solids`: a rock, a crate, a wall's end, nothing too big to get round) and holds it `coverHold` seconds, where a hit lands at `coverHit` of its chance; a soldier's goal is its squad's order first (a fall-back, a flank), then its cover, then its spot, and a side arrives at a post on its own side of it (`spotIn`'s `from`). `assaultScene.js` leans a soldier in when it's down or in cover.
- **Waves and a staging line**: a side's reinforcements come back together on a wave (`RULES.wave` for the attackers, `waveDefend` slower for the defenders), and the attackers' on a staging line `RULES.forward` metres short of their objective (`forwardOf`), not at a post a long walk from the fight; their first wave starts `firstWave` times as far back (1.5), and a map may set its own staging distance (`forward` in `assaults.js`: Endor's is 80 m where the rule's is 100, its posts far apart through the trees; with the rule's distance the first minute of Endor's battle was all walking and `assault-check.mjs` saw no contact in it), so a battle opens with their advance rather than a wait (Scarif's landing and Endor's village are 300 m from the front). With that the attackers can win: the tickets are equal at the start on every map (the phases still top the attackers up).
- **Merged with the squads** (`lib/ai/squad`, main's lane of the same day): a squad's nerve is read fresh each time from what it has against what faces it, with its side's recent losses counted (its last reading no longer feeds back in, which trapped a squad in panic until the field cleared, and swung every battle to whoever won the first exchange); a side out of tickets presses rather than holds; a soldier out of the fight heals (`RULES.heal`); a defender standing its ground inside a post its side holds is harder to hit (`RULES.dugIn`: the post is held, not stood on).
- **Balance, measured** with nobody playing (`stepBattle` from `chooseSide`, 0.1 s steps, an open field, the defenders' pools raised to 160–175 against the attackers' 100–110 plus the phases' top-ups): at the high tier's 14 a side the attackers win 8 of 16 runs across the four maps in 6 to 13 minutes (median about 10); at the mid tier's 9 a side 4 of 16, with 4 still running at 20 minutes (the smaller sides bleed slower). The player tilts it: with the attackers' pools equal to the defenders' the attackers won 12 of 16. Before the merge the attackers won 0 of 16; before the fresh reading, 13 of 16. With tickets of 10 against 300 the side with 10 loses on every map but one seed of Scarif's (`index.test.js` runs one seed of each).

## Checking it

- Tests: `npx vitest run src/components/galaxy/surface/missions`.
- In a browser: the dev server (`npx vite --port 5188`), then `OUT=/tmp/shots node scripts/assault-check.mjs hoth,geonosis,scarif,endor` (`SIDE=attack` to fight the other way). Screenshots: `assault-<id>-1-choose.png` … `5-lost.png`.
- The balance, in Node: a script over `ASSAULTS` that runs `newBattle` → `chooseSide(b, 'attack')` → `stepBattle` at 0.1 s with `createSolids()` and the site's reach until `b.result`, for a few seeds and both tiers' `SOLDIERS`; count who won and how long it took. `RULES` is a plain object, so a lever (`capture`, `forward`, `wave`) can be tried by assigning to it before the runs.
- By hand, in development: `window.__surface().mission` is the battle's view; `window.__surfaceDo('missionDo', 'side', 'attack')`, `window.__surfaceDo('missionDo', 'deploy', 'walkers')` (a post's id), `window.__surfaceDo('advance', 30)` runs thirty seconds without drawing, `window.__surfaceDo('missionDo', 'win')` / `'lose'` end it.
- Gotchas: `?quality=low` caps the soldiers at six a side (`SOLDIERS`); the posts' ids are the maps' (`missions/assaults.js`); the HUD's deploy card lists only your side's posts, disabled while the enemy's in them; the world's quests and places still work during a battle but their panel is hidden until it ends.

## The game’s deploy flow, and Hoth on the game’s own level (2026-10-11)

The design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-galactic-assault-bf2017-flow-design.md`; the decision: `docs/decisions/2026-10-11-one-deploy-flow-on-the-galaxy.md`. The owner asked for the 2017 game’s version, not the site’s older battle: the four classes, heroes by Battle Points, the game’s crosshair, figures that walk, and no white models. PR #933.

- **Hoth is the Battlefront world.** The galaxy’s mode menu card (`surface/modes.js`’s `BATTLEFRONT` row), Hoth’s panel tile (`GalaxyPanel.jsx`) and its briefing (`systems.js`, `game.also`) send the player to `/battlefront/hoth/galacticAssault?from=/galaxy/hoth`: the game’s level (Outpost Delta), its own loading, spawn, kill and end-of-round screens, HUD, crosshair, Battle Points and prices. `battlefront/index.js`’s `wayBack(search)` reads `from` (a galaxy world’s page alone), and the world’s menu and end of round lead back there. The spawn screen writes its offers as the game’s does (`hud/widgets.js`’s `offerNameOf`: the strings where the export has them, by hand for the six reinforcements and Anakin, in capitals).
- **The Battlefront world fetches its pack through the asset base** (`map/level.js` takes `packBytes` from the galaxy’s face, `galaxy/shared/level.js`): it fetched the site path alone, so once the packs left git for the bucket it drew nothing in a dev run (0 triangles against lane 5’s 448,469), and would on any host without the site’s copy.
- **The white.** `surface/level/levelGltf.js` bound a pack’s maps to the parser’s material alone; three r186’s GLTFLoader copies a material for a mesh with no normals or tangents, or with vertex colours, and the copy, the one drawn, stayed white; on the node renderer the game material was built from that white copy. It binds every copy now; a lost colour map leaves the material bare grey on the classic and the game material alike; `stats().maps` counts asked/loaded/failed with the first why, said once in the console, printed by `galaxy-check.mjs`. The KTX2 loader waits for a WebGPU renderer’s device before reading its formats (`lib/three/gltf.js`’s `detectSupport`).
- **The Battlefront world’s figures pace their clips to the sim** (`figures/locomotion.js`’s `clipSpeed` from each clip’s root travel, `rateFor` banded 0.5 to 1.8): walk 1.91 m/s, run 3.94, sprint 5.90, crouch.run 2.54 from `clips-humanoid.glb`; the sim’s 3.8 plays the run at 0.96, its 5.97 the sprint at 1.01. Strafes at full speed still slide a little (the pack has no run strafes; `C_HM_Rifle_Run_Left1` and kin would end it).
- **The other seven worlds** keep the galaxy’s engine (which already draws the game’s level packs and its figures) and take the game’s deploy flow on it: side with its emblem, then the game’s spawn screen (`AssaultSpawn.jsx`, `assaultSpawn.css`): the four classes free, the side’s enforcer (a death trooper, a Wookiee warrior, a super battle droid: `troopers.js`’s `ENFORCERS`) and its heroes at the record’s price (`missions/points.js` over `points.json`: `priceOf`, `offersFor`, `EARN`), in the muted ink until the Battle Points of this battle afford them; the posts beside it; Battle Points and the keys under. The rules (`assault.js`: `you.points`, `you.as`, `deploy(b, postId, offer)`) earn a kill 100, a post taken with you in it 200 (by hand), ten a second in a contested live post, spend at deploy, keep through deaths, reset with the battle. A class or enforcer pick is kept as the loadout, a bought hero lasts one life and going down puts the last class back (`missions/deployPick.js`, `pages/GalaxySurface.jsx`’s `onAssaultDeploy`). Left: no refund when a bought body fails to load; the super battle droid’s body is `rig: 'own'`, which `heroSpec` does not carry, so it may stand in.
- **The game’s crosshair on the surface** (`reticleFor.js`, `HitMarker.jsx`): a blaster draws the game’s dot and notches through the kit’s `Reticle shape=`, a hit its four ticks, a kill its longer bars in the warning colour; the ring over a target, on the HUD and in the world (`scene.js`’s `lockRing`), is the lightsaber’s alone.
- **The mode menu** (`ModeMenu.jsx`, `flow.css`, `modes.js`’s `film`): the game’s multiplayer screen, Galactic Assault the large tile with the game’s film, the rest in the grid, the keys at the foot.

### What moves a world onto the game’s own level

The Battlefront world needs of a level what the data holds for Hoth alone: a teams row (`src/data/bf2017/teams.json`: `Orig:hoth_01` only), a stages rulebook with its objectives chosen from the map’s volumes (`maps/endor.stages.json` has its three stages and empty objectives; `hoth.stages.json` and `NOTES.md` show how Hoth’s were read from `FantasyBattle_Logic`), a lighting rulebook (`maps/hoth.lighting.json` is the only one; the galaxy’s `light/<world>.json` records are another shape), its weather and picture (`light/<world>.picture.json` exists for endor, kashyyyk, geonosis, naboo, kamino, bespin, scarif). One level a lane, from the export on the owner’s desktop; Endor first.
