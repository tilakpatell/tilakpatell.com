# Hand-off: the universe map and the site’s shell

`/universe` is the whole site laid out as places to fly to: six stations round a sun for the portfolio pages, the fandoms’ planets further out, wonders in deep space, a gate to the Star Wars galaxy, a portal to the Rick and Morty sector, and the Expanse past the rim. You fly it in a crew’s own ship (the Falcon, the X-wing, Rick’s cruiser, the RV), with that crew’s enemies, allies and events round you, a nav map to pick a place and a drive, a hangar and shipyard to fit the ship, one economy across the map, the galaxy and the worlds, and other pilots in view. Round it sits the site’s shell: the tour that shows people round, each world’s basics, one house UI for the pages and the map’s HUD, and one feel (the house look, hits, springs, one tuning panel) across every world. This note merges the hand-offs for all of that into one.

## The owner’s rules

- Nothing a visitor can do is lost. Every key, save key (`tp-pilot`, `tp-gcw`, `tp-mc` copied, never deleted), achievement, sound, ghost and dev hook works after as before. A new sound is added, never swapped. Existing tests stay green and are not loosened.
- Pure first. `src/lib`, `src/runtime` and the generators import no React, no DOM and no three.js; every rule has its test in Node before its drawing half.
- No new dependency: no Tweakpane, no lil-gui, no runtime call to any service. A library gets its page under `docs/stack/` before it is imported.
- A changed picture is on purpose. Take the before shots on `main` first; the after goes beside them in the pull request. A glow lost under the bloom threshold is a missed emissive: lift it over 1 with `hot()`, never lower the threshold.
- Draw calls and triangles no higher at any checked route; say the numbers. Compare against your own before, not only the baseline.
- His numbers are the start, not the law: change a constant only when a test or a screenshot says to, and say which.
- One pull request a lane, merged on its own: CI green, a merge commit. Never merge red, never force-push, never rebase anyone’s branch. Before it: `npm run lint`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, `node scripts/health.mjs --check --skip build`, and the smoke check on your routes.
- Follow the spec over the plan, the code over both. Fix the plan’s line and say so in one sentence; a spec decision you disagree with is a finding, and the work goes on as written.
- Generated content is never stored: seeds and deltas only. The service worker is passive; if in doubt, make it do less. The map plays the same inside the rim.
- Every new `onBeforeCompile` needs a `customProgramCacheKey` and a place in `scene.js`’s warm-up, or it compiles mid-flight. `post.js` is shared with the galaxy: a new uniform defaults to leaving their picture as it was.
- The showroom shows the iconic ships’ own models, not plain hulls. When a garage build flies in place of the crew’s ship, the panel says so. Apply tries every fit before it pays. The ion burst and the Mk II torpedoes are priced by hand.
- The nav map’s stylesheet is words only; its pilots’ colour and ambers are the owner’s call. “Super speed” keeps its name; its verb is “Race”. The HUD’s key cap is flat; the site’s raised one is for the light pages. Depth tokens name today’s numbers; nothing reorders.
- No model names in code, docs or commits. British spelling, curly quotes, plain short sentences; comments say why. Keep output terse.
