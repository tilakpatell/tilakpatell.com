# Hand-off: Battlefront

The Battlefront world is the 2017 game’s modes played on the site from the game’s own data. The owner’s export of the game (gameplay records as EBX JSON, MeshSets, clips, textures, maps, Havok shapes) sits on their desktop and in the private `bf2017-assets` bucket; the pipeline’s scripts under `scripts/` (named `bf2017-`) read it into rulebooks (`src/data/bf2017/`), models, clips, level packs and the game’s front end. The rules run as a pure sim (`src/lib/battlefront/`) that the world (`src/components/battlefront/`) draws; Hoth’s Galactic Assault is the first battle, reached at `/battlefront/hoth` through the game’s own menu. Beside it the galaxy’s surfaces take the game’s levels, cast, vehicles, light and deploy flow, the sabers fence on the game’s hitboxes and stroke tables, and the combat (look, bolts, blades, duellists) is the ground all of it stands on. This page merges the former hand-offs for the game, the asset pipeline, the physics, the canvas front end, Galactic Assault, Hoth’s map, the saber forms and the combat revamp.
