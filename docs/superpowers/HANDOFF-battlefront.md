# Hand-off: Battlefront

The Battlefront world is the 2017 game’s modes played on the site from the game’s own data. The owner’s export of the game (gameplay records as EBX JSON, MeshSets, clips, textures, maps, Havok shapes) sits on their desktop and in the private `bf2017-assets` bucket; the pipeline’s scripts under `scripts/` (named `bf2017-`) read it into rulebooks (`src/data/bf2017/`), models, clips, level packs and the game’s front end. The rules run as a pure sim (`src/lib/battlefront/`) that the world (`src/components/battlefront/`) draws; Hoth’s Galactic Assault is the first battle, reached at `/battlefront/hoth` through the game’s own menu. Beside it the galaxy’s surfaces take the game’s levels, cast, vehicles, light and deploy flow, the sabers fence on the game’s hitboxes and stroke tables, and the combat (look, bolts, blades, duellists) is the ground all of it stands on. This page merges the former hand-offs for the game, the asset pipeline, the physics, the canvas front end, Galactic Assault, Hoth’s map, the saber forms and the combat revamp.

## Left

- **A first visit a quarter smaller.** Cloudflare compresses the engine’s wasm and JSON itself (104 MB of wasm arrives as 17), but not the textures (1.65 GB), the scene data (0.3 GB) or the collision. `scripts/bf2web-compress.mjs` writes brotli copies of those into the bucket, with an `encodings.json` listing them, and `cloudflare/play.js` sends a copy to a browser that takes brotli (whole requests only: the engine asks ranges of the menu videos alone). Measured on samples, brotli 9: textures to 85%, scene data 33%, collision 18%; about 2.07 GB to 1.57 on the wire. The pipeline compresses every build it adopts (`stage: compress` alone for one). A repeat visit still leans on the browser’s HTTP cache, which may drop 2.2 GB: bf2-web’s own OPFS cache (`runtime/payload-cache.js`) isn’t wired into the play page.
- **Why frame 0 never came, and who is sent to the engine now.** Headless on the live site (2026-10-28) the engine loaded to 88% and then waited on frame 0 for good: 12 of its 367 pipelines failed (`Invalid PipelineLayout`), the render thread stopped submitting. Ten of the materials Hoth draws sample 23 textures in one pixel stage; WebGPU’s default `maxSampledTexturesPerShaderStage` is 16, SwiftShader’s adapter allows 16, so their layouts can’t be made. The owner’s laptop (no dedicated GPU) failed at frame 0 the same way. The adopt step now reads each build’s needs off the programs it draws (`programNeeds`, over `pipelines.json`’s names) into `play.json`’s `needs`, and `engineSupport` sends to the engine only an adapter with them (main-da7a51b3: 23 textures, 8 samplers, 7 storage buffers, 1 storage texture, 3 uniform buffers, 4 groups, dual-source blending); everyone else gets the site’s world. bf2-web could reach more GPUs by binding fewer textures (an array, or a split) in those ten.
- **bf2-web’s builds, on their own** ([the decision](../decisions/2026-10-28-bf2web-pipeline.md)). `.github/workflows/bf2web.yml` builds `bf2-web`’s main on the desktop (`scripts/desktop/bf2web-build.mjs`, set `BF2WEB_REPO` in the runner’s `.env`), uploads what changed (`scripts/bf2web-upload.mjs`), holds the newest build to the contract (`scripts/bf2web-adopt.mjs`, `scripts/lib/bf2web-site.mjs`), opens it through the Worker’s code (`scripts/bf2web-smoke.mjs`), pushes `play.json` and starts the deploy. Waiting on `bf2-web`: its `WebPort/deploy/build-play-site.mjs` with a checked-in recipe, `feat/deploy-bundle` on its main, and its notify workflow’s `SITE_DISPATCH_TOKEN`; until then the build stage stops and says so, and adopt works on builds uploaded by hand.
- **Hoth on `bf2-web`, next.** `src/data/bf2web/play.json` names the first build, `main-da7a51b3` (`bf2017-assets/sites/main-da7a51b3/`, 3,780 files, 2.2 GB, uploaded and checked by the owner’s desktop on 2026-10-28; a visitor is asked before it downloads, `EngineChoice.jsx`, the answer kept as `tp-bf2-engine`). Its `/export/` is the bucket’s `web/` (the export `BF2_EXPORT_ROOT` names; `DEPLOY.md`’s `extract/` copy isn’t needed); its `/ui-data/` is `library/ui/frontend/`, the nine front-end rulebooks, written by `bf2-web`’s `frontend/tools/extract.mjs` from the bucket’s `UI/**` and `Gameplay/Profiles/**` records and uploaded on 2026-10-28 (84 of those records came down from the bucket already unzipped under a `.json.gz` name; zip them again before running it).
