# Beyond WebGL: what the named projects do, what three r186 gives us, and how AI can run the people and the animals

Date: 2026-10-06. The owner's brief: “Everything is saying to move beyond Three.js and use WebGPU and write GLSL shaders”, with seven projects to look at and a request to use AI for conversational NPCs and alien life. This note is what was found. The design that follows from it is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-webgpu-worlds-and-living-worlds-design.md`.

Read with: `docs/research/2026-10-04-webgl-portfolios.md` (how the strong WebGL portfolios are built), `docs/research/2026-10-06-bruno-simon-folio.md` (the 2025 folio is TSL on WebGPU), `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-world-runtime-design.md` (the runtime already has a WebGPU backend).

## The short version

1. **“Beyond Three.js” means beyond `WebGLRenderer`, not beyond three.** The projects that left three behind (Kosmos, Rezcraft) each wrote a whole engine. This site has 1,483 source files, 133 files that make a `ShaderMaterial`, 75 that patch one with `onBeforeCompile`, 478 GLB models and a world runtime with loaders, caches, a quality controller and a WebGPU backend that nothing uses yet. The move is to three's `WebGPURenderer` (`three/webgpu`) and its shading language TSL, which compiles to WGSL on WebGPU and to GLSL on the WebGL 2 fallback.
2. **WebGPU does not run GLSL.** It runs WGSL. Hand-written shaders stay possible in three r186 through `wgslFn` (a WGSL function as a node) and `glslFn` (its GLSL twin for the fallback backend); both verified in `node_modules/three/src/nodes/code/FunctionNode.js`. Anything that must draw on both backends is written once in TSL; the heavy procedural kernels (terrain height, an atmosphere march, a nebula march) can be hand-written WGSL with a cheaper TSL stand-in for WebGL.
3. **The references split into three families**: a procedural universe (Kosmos, COSMOS), the real Earth from tiles (geo-three, openglobus, OpenSkyFlight), and a from-scratch engine (Rezcraft). Each lends something concrete, listed below.
4. **AI for the people and the animals is two different problems.** Conversation needs a model at runtime (a Worker-proxied Claude, or an in-browser model on WebGPU, with baked lines as the floor). Alien life does not: a species can be designed once, ahead of time, by Claude, and built and animated procedurally from its sheet, which keeps the site's rule that nothing is fetched from an AI service to make an asset at runtime.

## three r186, as installed here, verified in `node_modules/three`

- `three/webgpu` exports `WebGPURenderer`; `forceWebGL: true` picks its WebGL 2 backend (`src/renderers/webgpu/WebGPURenderer.js`). The backend is `src/renderers/webgl-fallback/WebGLBackend.js`: it runs compute as transform feedback with one count only, no indirect dispatch (warned as unsupported), no storage-texture writes. **Compute that writes storage buffers or textures, and indirect draws, are WebGPU only** (`WebGPUBackend.js` has `drawIndexedIndirect`, `IndirectStorageBufferAttribute`).
- TSL: `Fn`, `wgslFn`, `glslFn`, `wgsl`, `glsl`, `code` in `src/nodes/code/`; node materials for every classic material (`MeshStandardNodeMaterial`, `MeshPhysicalNodeMaterial`, `MeshMatcapNodeMaterial`, `SpriteNodeMaterial`, `PointsNodeMaterial`, `VolumeNodeMaterial`…) in `src/materials/nodes/`; `logarithmicDepthBuffer` and `reversedDepthBuffer` options on the common `Renderer`.
- Post as nodes in `examples/jsm/tsl/display/`: `BloomNode`, `GTAONode`, `SSAONode`, `SSGINode`, `SSRNode`, `DepthOfFieldNode`, `LensflareNode`, `Lut3DNode`, `FilmNode`, `FXAANode`, `SMAANode`, `TRAANode`, `TAAUNode`, `FSR1Node`, `GodraysNode`, `MotionBlur`, `ChromaticAberrationNode`, `OutlineNode`, `PixelationPassNode`, `TransitionNode` and more. The runtime's `webgpu.js` already builds `render` and `bloom` from a description.
- The runtime (`src/runtime/`): `pickBackend` gives WebGPU only to a module that declares `shading: 'nodes'`; `shading.test.js` fails the build if such a module's folder makes a `ShaderMaterial`, patches with `onBeforeCompile` or builds an `EffectComposer`. No world declares `'nodes'` yet. A lost device falls back to WebGL once.

### Probed in this container's headless Chromium (Playwright 1.56's build, Chromium 141)

- `navigator.gpu` is **absent on `about:blank`** and **present on an `http://` origin** (WebGPU needs a secure context; `127.0.0.1` counts). With `--headless=new --enable-unsafe-webgpu --enable-features=Vulkan,WebGPU --use-vulkan=swiftshader --use-angle=vulkan --ignore-gpu-blocklist --enable-unsafe-swiftshader`, `requestAdapter()` returns a software adapter with 19 features. So a nodes world **can be checked in CI** on software WebGPU, as COSMOS was.
- three r186's `WebGPURenderer` on that adapter threw on its first frame: `Failed to execute 'createView' on 'GPUTexture': Failed to read the 'swizzle' property`. three r186 passes the texture-view `swizzle` field; Chrome added it in **143** (November 2025). Chromium 141 is older. Two consequences: the check script needs a Playwright whose Chromium is 143 or newer; and a visitor on an old Chrome would hit the same error after `init()` succeeded, so the runtime needs a **first-frame guard** (a throw in the first draw downgrades to WebGL, as a lost device does).
- The same scene on `forceWebGL: true` drew correctly (centre pixel 189, 186, 188 for a uv-coloured plane). TSL on the fallback works.

## AI for the people (conversation)

Three ways to put a model behind an NPC on a static site, and what each costs. The design uses all three in order: remote, then local, then baked.

| | How | Cost to the visitor | Cost to the owner | Quality | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Remote (Claude through a Cloudflare Worker)** | The page POSTs the persona id and the transcript to a Worker; the Worker holds `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` as a secret, checks the origin, counts per-IP and global daily calls in KV, calls the Messages API with the persona's system prompt cached, returns `{ say, mood, action }` as structured output. DRIFTWING's pattern. Cloudflare's free tier covers the Worker (100k requests a day) and KV. | Nothing to download; ~1–3 s a line. | Per line, with a ~600-token cached persona and ~120 output tokens: Claude Opus 5.5 ≈ $0.003 (cache reads $0.20/MTok, output $20/MTok); Sonnet 5.5 ≈ $0.0014; Haiku 4.5 ≈ $0.0007. A global cap of 2,000 lines a day bounds the bill at about $6 a day on Opus. | Best. Knows the world from the prompt; keeps the owner's rules. | Needs one piece of infrastructure outside GitHub Pages (a Worker). Abuse bounded by the caps. |
| **Local (WebLLM on WebGPU)** | `@mlc-ai/web-llm` 0.2.85 (September 2026) has 163 prebuilt models. Small ones: SmolLM2-360M (204 MB download, ~376 MB VRAM), Qwen3-0.6B (335 MB, ~1.4 GB VRAM), Llama 3.2 1B (695 MB, ~880 MB VRAM). Cached after the first download; works offline. | A download the size of two worlds, once, gated like `WORLD_MB`; a few seconds to load; desktop with WebGPU only. | None. | Rough at 360M–600M: short lines, forgets, drifts off persona. Good enough for small talk; not for a character that should know the site. | Memory on phones; the shading test's budgets. |
| **Baked** | Lines written ahead (as the crews' 167 lines in `scripts/voices/`), picked by a tested rule from the situation; the voices pipeline records them in the character's voice. | Nothing. | Nothing at runtime. | Exact, in character, voiced. | Finite. |

Voice: real-time cloned voices are not possible on a static site. The browser's `speechSynthesis` (as `rickmorty/world/shipVoice.js`) says a generated line with captions; baked lines keep their recorded voices.

## AI for the animals (alien life)

- **Design offline, build at runtime, from a sheet.** A script (`scripts/species.mjs`, run on the owner's machine with the key in `.env.local`) asks Claude for a *species sheet* per seed: body plan (one of six), size, limb count and placement, colours, gait, temperament, diet, call, home biome, a name. Claude returns it as structured output against a schema; the sheet is committed as JSON. Nothing at runtime talks to an AI service to make a creature, which is the standing rule.
- **The body is procedural.** As `threejs-procedural-animals` does: a body plan is a parametric builder (a spine of capsules, limbs placed by the sheet, a head), auto-rigged (the repo has `lib/three/rig.js` and `ik.js`), gaits as IK driven by speed, skinned on the GPU. A herd is one instanced draw per species with the gait phase in the vertex node (TSL). On WebGPU, flocking can run in compute; on WebGL it runs on the CPU for a smaller herd.
- **Behaviour is rules.** Graze, flock, flee, approach-when-curious, call-and-answer: a tested `rules.js` with the sheet's temperament as its parameters.
- **Talking animals are NPCs.** An alien that can be spoken to is a persona like any other; its sheet is the persona's facts.

## What fits this site, judged

| Idea | Fit (5 best) | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| three `WebGPURenderer` + TSL for new worlds, GLSL worlds untouched | 5 | The runtime already gates it; a world at a time; nothing a visitor can do is lost. |
| A shared `src/lib/tsl/` (noise, fbm, atmosphere, blackbody, dither, matcap, the grade) | 5 | The same functions exist in GLSL in `lib/three/noise.js`, `galaxy/bodyShaders.js`, `universe/post.js`; ported once, every nodes world shares them. |
| Hand-written WGSL through `wgslFn` for the procedural kernels | 4 | The brief asks for hand-written shaders; WGSL is what WebGPU runs. Each needs a TSL or `glslFn` stand-in while the WebGL fallback must draw it. |
| A procedural star system beyond the map's edge (COSMOS/Kosmos) | 5 | The universe is the site's signature; the map's planets are all fandom worlds, and a frontier of worlds nobody drew is the one thing the map lacks. Compute terrain and atmospheres are what WebGPU is for. |
| Alien life from Claude-designed sheets | 4 | New, cheap at runtime, keeps the asset rule. |
| Conversational NPCs through a Worker | 4 | Real conversation; one small piece of infrastructure and a cap. |
| WebLLM on-device | 2 | Optional, desktop only, big download, weak at the sizes a page can afford. Keep the seam, ship it last if at all. |
| Earth from tiles at runtime (OpenSkyFlight) | 2 | Against the asset rule and Esri's terms. Baked tiles for the Travel page's places with CC-BY imagery (EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless) is the version that fits: owner's call on the bytes. |
| Leaving three (Kosmos, Rezcraft) | 1 | A whole engine for no gain the renderer swap does not give. |
| Raw WebGPU without three | 1 | Same. |
