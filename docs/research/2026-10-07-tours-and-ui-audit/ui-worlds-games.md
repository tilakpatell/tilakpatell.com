# The worlds' game UI: HUDs, prompts, menus, touch controls

An audit of every HUD over a 3D world or game on tilakpatell.com, read from the source on 2026-10-07. Scope: the walkable and flyable worlds (Albuquerque, Avengers HQ, Cybertron, Dot Matrix, Earth, the galaxy and its surfaces, Invincible, Mario 64, Middle-earth's Shire and eleven towns, Minecraft, the music courtyard, Scranton, Dimension C-137, the Citadel, the universe map) and the games that open inside or beside them (Dead Man's Tide, the trench run, the N64 and Eaglercraft frames, the eight Avengers HQ games, Roll Out, Portal Panic, Roy, the Sewer, the Rush). Every claim carries a `path:line`.

The owner's view going in: the game frontends mostly look good; spacing is the likely issue; and there should not be several different components or words for one thing. The audit agrees with the first two and finds the third is the biggest problem: the same HUD idea is built between seven and fourteen times, each with its own words, sizes and offsets.

## 1. What is good, and what to standardise on

**The shared runtime gives a world a canvas and nothing else.** `src/runtime/WorldHost.jsx:6-11` is a box that takes the page's HUD as children; `src/runtime/runtime.css:1-18` styles only the host, the canvas and the handover snapshot. The handoff says so too: "the page's HUD, labels and controls go over it as children" (`src/runtime/WorldHost.jsx:2-3`). So the runtime is not where HUD consistency comes from today; nothing shared sits between the runtime and the sixteen HUDs.

**Pieces that already are shared, and work:**

- `src/components/guide/GuideCue.jsx:3-9`: the one suffix on every world's first hint (" · ? all the controls"). Used by 19 files (Albuquerque, Avengers, Dot Matrix, Earth, the galaxy view, the Shire and eight towns, the music courtyard, Scranton, C-137, the Citadel, the universe map). Not used by Invincible, Cybertron, Mario 64, Minecraft, the galaxy surface page, the Caribbean or the trench run.
- `src/components/guide/KeyTable.jsx:7-40`: one way of drawing keys, used by the guide panel and the tour.
- `src/components/tour/briefs.js:30-700`: one briefing component (the tour's `kind: 'brief'` cards, `src/components/tour/Tour.jsx:48-51`: "The basics", "Skip the basics", "Let's go") for 19 routes. This is the right pattern for a mission briefing and should be the only one.
- `src/components/middleearth/towns/TownHud.jsx`: `QuestList` (15-70), `Bubble` (93-103), `Convo` (108-143), `Stick` (146-154), `Travellers` (158-171). One HUD, re-skinned by CSS variables in 17 places (`--shire-glass`, `--shire-gold`, `--shire-line` redefined in `src/components/office/world/world.css:5-12`, `src/components/rickmorty/citadel/citadel.css:5-12`, `src/components/middleearth/towns/bree/bree.css:5`, and every other town). The Shire, eleven towns, Scranton and the Citadel all share `shire.css`'s one set of positions. This is the proof that the "one HUD, many skins" model works on this site.
- `src/components/games/games.css:47-84`: a shared game frame (`.g3`, `.g3-hud`, `.g3-overlay`, `.g3-touch`, `.g3-touch-btn` 60 px, `.g3-tools` top 10 right 10, `.g3-callout`, `.g3-keys`). Used by three games only: `DeadMansTide.jsx`, `RollOut.jsx`, `PortalPanic.jsx`.
- `src/components/avengers/hq/hq.css:12-26`: one overlay (title, text, score, best, keys, stone) for the eight HQ games, with the keys list hidden on touch (`:22`).
- `src/components/games/pad.js:8-48`: one gamepad reader. There is no touch-stick equivalent; every world draws and reads its own stick (section 6).

**The reference HUD: Invincible.** `src/components/invincible/world/hud.js:1-54` is the only HUD whose layout is decided by pure, tested rules: `layoutCompass` keeps names 72 px apart and off the strip's right end where the buttons sit (`:10`, `:18-34`); `titleMode` turns the title into a chip 2.5 s after the first move or at once when there is an objective (`:38`); `objectiveText` formats "step · 310 m" (`:43-47`); `markerSize` never shrinks a target marker under 24 px (`:51-54`). `InvHud.jsx:1-147` holds the markup out of the world file; the frame loop writes numbers straight into refs (`:28`) rather than re-rendering; the top row is a time chip and **one Menu** (`:41-62`) with time, controls, other players and the way out, closing on a click elsewhere or Escape (`:16-27`); the objective sits centred under the compass (`world.css:56`); the compass and map follow the measured bottom of the buttons however they wrap (`--iw-under`, `InvWorld.jsx:776`, `world.css:81-82`); the canvases are sized at device pixel ratio (`InvWorld.jsx:756-768`); the bottom row keeps an explicit clearance for the site's "?" button and the map (`world.css:49`); the map moves to the top on a phone, clear of the thumbs (`world.css:130-136`); the prompt is a pill with the key first, "E Burger Mart" (`InvHud.jsx:116-118`, `world.css:124`). That is the shape to copy: one tested `hud.js` of rules, one `Hud.jsx` of markup, one Menu, one objective line, one prompt, measured stacking.

What Invincible does not do, and the standard should: it has no first-move hint and no `GuideCue` (no match for `hint` or `GuideCue` in `InvWorld.jsx`); its "Controls" opens its own dialog (`InvHud.jsx:68-92`) rather than the site's guide, so the keys are written twice (`src/components/guide/pages.js:224`); its touch row uses a magic 9.5 rem bottom padding (`world.css:139`); and its map on a phone sits at `--iw-under + 112px` (`world.css:133`), another magic number.

## 9. The standard, in one list

What every world's HUD should be, taken from the parts that already work:

1. One `Hud.jsx` per world, over `WorldHost`, laid out by a shared tested `hud.js` (Invincible's shape).
2. Top row: title (collapses to a chip once moving), objective under it on the left; counters and one **Menu** on the right. Menu holds Settings, Controls (the site's guide), Things to do, Players, Universe map.
3. One objective component, one prompt component (pill, key first: "E Go in · Burger Mart"), one toast at top centre, one bubble (`TownHud`'s), one list (`QuestList`), one place frame, one players chip, one `kbd` (`.guide-kbd`).
4. Spacing tokens in `runtime.css`: `--hud-pad: clamp(0.75rem, 2vw, 1.25rem)`, `--hud-pad-b: max(var(--hud-pad), env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`, `--guide-reserve: calc(16px + 42px + 0.9rem)`, `--hud-foot` measured from the touch row, `--z-place`.
