# The Battlefront rulebooks: the hand values

Everything under `src/data/bf2017/` is written by `node scripts/bf2017-data.mjs all --root <export> --level hoth_01 --era Orig` from the 2017 game’s data, each number with a `_source` naming its record, except the hand files and hand values below, which carry `"source": "hand"`. One line per hand value: what it is, why it is by hand, and where to look to replace it.

## `maps/hoth.stages.json`

The stage order and objectives of Hoth’s Galactic Assault are a logic graph in the game (`PF_GameMode_Conquest_Staged`, `FantasyBattle_Logic`’s `Delay`, `Compare` and `IntHub` nodes), readable by a person and not by a script. What the data does say, and this file follows:

- **Three stages**, not four: the strings name `STAGE1` to `STAGE3`, each with a line per team (`TAKE OUT THE WALKERS` / `TAKE OUT ANY THREATS TO THE AT-ATS`; `STOP THE IMPERIALS FROM ENTERING THE BASE` / `ADVANCE AND SECURE THE HANGAR AREA`; `HOLD OFF THE IMPERIAL ATTACKERS UNTIL OUR ESCAPE` / `STOP THE REBELS FROM ESCAPING`). The ids are `walkers`, `hangars`, `escape`.
- **walkers**: two escorts, one per walker path: `FantasyBattle_Logic:123` starts beside the AT-AT spawner `FantasyBattle_Logic:59` and `:303` beside `:208`, both ending at the hangar gates. The six `uplink` objectives are the six 3 m-high volumes in `FantasyBattle_Logic` along the paths, and they are **not the game’s**: the graph links all six to one `PF_FireDMG` (`FantasyBattle_Gameplay:99`, a `WSDamageAreaTriggerEntityData` of 10 a second that spares Team1), so they are the field’s fires, and Hoth’s Galactic Assault has no uplink prefab at all (bf2-re findings/63 §7). The Rebels’ own stage-1 tool in the records is the HH-12 ion disruptor in the eleven `PF_Pickup_ObjectiveBomb` (findings/63 §6); what it does to a walker is not read yet. The uplinks, their 6 s hold, 250 m range, 30 s run and rest and 20 % bombing run (`modes/objectives.js`) stay, by hand, as the site’s stand-in for it until it is.
- **hangars**: the two 10 m-high volumes `FantasyBattle_Shapes:33` and `:68` sit behind the two hangar gates (the gate spheres `FantasyBattle_Shapes:73` and `:23`, radius 12.09 m, each with a `PF_Hoth_Destruction_Gate` and a `PF_CapturePoint`: `FantasyBattle_Logic:209` and `:134`). Which is east and which west is a guess: east is the larger X (251 m against 179 m), taking +X as east. Swap the two `name`s if the game’s compass says otherwise.
- **escape**: the three `PF_Interact_PlanetaryBattle_Bomb` prefabs deep in the base (`FantasyBattle_Logic:4`, `:131`, `:63`, west to east) take the names the level wires into their `ObjectiveName`: `:4` the station, `:131` the depot, `:63` the pipes (`_FUEL_SILO` reads “FUEL PIPES”); the map rulebook’s `charges` rows carry them, and the objectives take theirs.
- **spawns**: no longer played on Hoth: the map rulebook's `spawnLogic.galacticAssault` is the sub-world's own graph (below), and `modes/galacticAssault.js` takes its sets wherever the stages are the level's own. The hand sets stay for a test's cut-down stage list. What they were: each stage’s spawn areas (`SpawnLocationFinderShapeData`, Empire team 2 attacking, Rebels team 1 defending) chosen by how far the front has come, on the Z of each area’s centre: walkers, attack Z −900 to −1150, defend −1150 to −1420; hangars, attack −1150 to −1420, defend −1420 to −1600; escape, attack −1420 to −1720, defend −1600 to −1720. The data’s own switch is each area’s `Enabled` flag, set by the graph; read it there to replace the bands. The drawing behind these choices is `docs/superpowers/evidence/battlefront-lane0/hoth-layout.svg`.
- `vehicles`: the AT-AT in the walkers stage, from the map’s `vehicleSpawns`.

## `points.json`

Written by `node scripts/bf2017-data.mjs points` from `Online/BattlepointCostData` (lane G3; it was lane 0's hand table). The record holds the prices, not the earnings:

- **Read**: `cost.heroes` (`HeroCostConfig`, `EraCost`; every hero 4,000; `default` is the most common), `cost.units` (the default price list, `UnitCostConfig` with `PLANETARY_BATTLES` and an empty `Map`; keyed by the rulebooks' ids where they hold the unit), `cost.other` (OT and PT units the rulebooks do not hold yet, by the record's names), `cost.byMode.<mode>.<level key>` (each map's own table: Hoth's A-wing 200 and AT-ST 800, the Ewok 1,500 on every OT map; Kessel's and Jabba's Palace's Extraction tables), `settings.<mode>` (the names that are no unit of the default list: spawn times, round times, HvV's 35 tickets and `HvV_ENABLE_*`, Strike's bomb timers, the sabers' stamina costs under `global`). The sequel era's 25 names (heroes, units, maps) are refused.
- **A reading**: the record's `ATAT` (400 on Hoth) is priced on `Kit_Vehicle_ATAT_Objective`, the team's AT-AT kit (the walker's guns a player takes); the escort walker (`Vehicle_Ground_AT-AT_MP`) is the mode's, not bought.
- **By hand** (`source: "hand"`), the record holding none: `earn` (`kill` 100, `assist` 50, `vehicleKill` 150, `heroKill` 250, `objectiveTick` 10 a second, `objectiveDamage` 1, `squadSpawn` 25: the game as played; `Prefabs/GameplaySupply/PF_Gameplay_BattlePoints_Local` is the graph to read them from), `limits` (`heroesPerTeam` 1, `reinforcementsPerTeam` 4; `kits.json` holds the graph's own per-kit limits), `bpRate` (1: `InstantActionBPRate`'s default).

## `kits.json`

Written by `node scripts/bf2017-data.mjs kits` from `PF_KitLimitations_PlanetaryBattles`, `_HeroesVSVillains`, `_TrooperModes`, `_Skirmish`, `_Skirmish_Space` (lane G3). Each `KitLimitationEntityData` row's `KitId` is the `Identifier` of the kit's gameplay asset (`GP_*`: `GP_Hero_DarthVader` is 518018118), so a row is named by fetching every `*CustomizationGameplayAsset` record. Its `MaxCount`, `Price` and `EnabledAtStage` are field ids the prefab's `PropertyConnections` bind; each is read back up the graph (`bf2017-rulebook-costs.mjs` says how: interface defaults, `PF_HeroKitMaxCount` (a bool cast to 1), `PF_SpecialsKitMaxCount` (its `Int32 4` through an `IntHub`), the `MathEntityData` select, the record's prices). **Resolved**: Galactic Assault 59 of 60 limits and 60 of 60 prices (81 rows, 21 the sequel era's: the shared matcher misses the First Order's and the Resistance's kit names, so `SEQUEL_KITS` names them); all prefabs 103 limits and 115 prices of 168 rows (51 sequel rows left out).

- A debug override (`DynamicDebugVariableEntityData`) is read as unset (true).
- **By hand**, where the chain ends in a node not read (Skirmish's limits come from the Arcade's settings through a `MathEntityData` input; `SelectIntEntityData`): `{ source: "hand", value, hash }` with the game as played (a hero 1, a reinforcement 4) and `null` for a vehicle (no as-played number); the hash is the field to follow.

## `arcade.json`

Written by `node scripts/bf2017-data.mjs arcade` (lane G3, `bf2017-arcade.mjs`): the battle scenarios (`PF_Skirmish_BattleScenario_Teams_Scenario_*`, 18 of 24 kept: the six with a NewEra side are refused), each side's team record, faction, era, heroes and specials (team records' names: `teams.json` holds no Skirmish team); the 22 `SkirmishParam_*` (choices and the default; `DefaultIntValue` is read as an index, since `Team1ScoreLimit`'s 0 is none of its values); `SkirmishBaseParameters`' order; the bots' names (Empire, Republic, Separatists, and the Rebels' list, which the Resistance shares; the First Order's refused). Every scenario's planet list is empty in the data. Onslaught's and Duel's limits are not records: `PF_Skirmish_GameModeText_*` are the HUD's announcements (`LAST STAND`, `ONE KILL REMAINING`); the limits are the settings (`GameModePreset`, the score and time limits).

## Read, not by hand, but worth knowing

- Weapon names are the strings `ID_W_<id>`; class names `ID_C_<CLASS>_TROOPER` or `ID_C_<CLASS>` (the Specialist has neither: its name is empty); hero names `ID_CHAR_<HERO>`. Ability and star card names follow no key the data shows and are left out.
- Vader’s armour levels read 850, 875, 900, 950 (`Affector_Health_DarthVader_VaderArmor1..4`).
- A mount’s own health is 1 (`WSMountHealthComponentData`): its rider takes the hits.

## `held.json` (the weapons in hand: `node scripts/bf2017-held.mjs --root lab/assets/bf2017`)

Every number is read; these are the hand parts around them.
- **`stance`** is a hand label: the weapon’s `AnimBaseSet` to the stance pack the site packed for it (`wabsRif` → `t`, `wabsPstl` → `p`, `wabsLMG` → `l`: `lib/three/walrusSets/stance.js`’s keys). The game picks its anim set by that name too; the pairing is ours.
- **The muzzle is the bone, the flash its effect’s offset.** The game spawns a bolt at the camera and draws it from `Wep_Muzzle` (the firing records’ `Shot.WeaponBone`, `SpawnVisualAtWeaponBone`), so the bolts start at `muzzle`. On the A280 (11 mm up, 8.5 mm out), DH-17 (26 mm down) and RK-3 (55 mm down) the flash sits off the bone; it is kept for the flash.
- **The converge distance** a drawn bolt takes to slide from the gun onto the sim’s line (`battlefront/fx/bolts.js`’s `CONVERGE`, 15 m) and **the catch-up time** it takes to reach the sim’s bolt, which the sim has a step out when it is first seen (`CATCH_UP`, 0.1 s), are by hand: the game hides the gap behind its camera spawn and the bolt’s speed, and no record holds a number for either.
- **How long a stopped bolt stays drawn** (`battlefront/battle.js`’s `SPENT`, 0.15 s, three of the sim’s steps): a bolt a wall, a body or its range stops is kept in the page’s view, `ended` where it stopped, so the drawing reaches it from the gun; most stop in the step they’re fired (a rifle’s 700 m/s is 35 m a step), and with the nav mask’s walls on Hoth more of them do. By hand, past `CATCH_UP`; a stopped bolt slides onto the sim’s line by where it stopped when that is nearer than `CONVERGE`.
- **The figure’s turn** (`battlefront/figures/facing.js`): the packed clips face +X because the packer drops `AITrajectory`; the clips’ own constant on that node, −90° about y, is put back on each soldier’s body.

## `maps/sb_endor.stages.json`

Endor’s space level (`Levels/Space/SB_Endor_01`) says its Starfighter Assault plainly: `SpaceBattle_Gameplay`’s `SpaceBattleObjectiveListEntityData` lists the phases in order (`Phase 1 - Corvettes`, `Phase 2 - Mines`, `Phase 3 - MC80`, then an empty `Intermission`), each with its objectives by name, the attacker (`Team2`, the Empire) and the defender (`Team1`, the Rebels), and its side objectives (the TIE bomber flights). The map row keeps it as `spaceBattle`. What the hand file adds:

- **Five stages, from three phases**: the MC80’s phase is three in the game’s strings (`…_PHASE_3` the top, `…_PHASE_3B` from beneath, `…_PHASE_3C` the rear engines), so the stages are `corvettes`, `mines`, `top`, `beneath`, `engines`, the last breaking the MC80 up.
- **corvettes**: `CR90_A`, `_B`, `_C` are the three `PF_CorvetteCR90_01` in `SpaceBattle_SecondaryObjective_Cruisers` (`:69`, `:377`, `:875`), in the layer’s order; each sinks its corvette.
- **mines**: the six `PF_Endor_SpaceBattles_Transmitter_Objective` in `SpaceBattle_ScriptedEvents` (named `REBEL MINE` in the prefab) take `Destroy Mine A` to `F` in the layer’s order: a guess.
- **top, beneath, engines**: the MC80 target prefabs (`Objectives/PF_SpaceBattles_MC80_target_*`) each hold one part of the MC80 the mode’s sub-level places (`TL01` the large nodule 01, `TL02` 02, `TR01` 04, `TR02` 03, `bottom_front` 05, `bottom_left` 06, `bottom_right` the nodule hangar, the final stage’s engines the engine target collision); `MC80 A1` to `A4` and `B1` to `B3` take them in that order: a guess.
- **gates and hp** (`opensAt` 0, 150, 270, 360, 440 s; hp 210, 240, 200, 170, 160): hand, so a battle the AI fights alone runs its ten minutes about as the galaxy’s battles do (`universe/battlePlan.js`’s `PLAN`).
- **bombers**: four of the eleven TIE bomber flights, one a stage, three bombers each (the layer’s `Vehicle_Air_TieBomber` spawns stand in threes), at hand times.
- **camera**: the level’s intro and outro cameras are Cinematics tracks (`Partition_*` `GroupTrackRootData`), not `CameraEntityData`; the hand camera is behind the Star Destroyer, looking at the MC80.
- **fighters**: the level’s own classes as the galaxy’s kinds: X-wing, A-wing, Y-wing; TIE fighter, TIE interceptor, TIE bomber.

## Heroes vs Villains and Blast (`maps/arenas.json`, `surface/missions/hvv.js`, `blast.js`)

`arenas.json` is cut from the five ground maps by `scripts/bf2017-arenas.mjs` (every number the rulebooks', checked by `arenas.test.js`). What the two modes add by hand, until lane D2 reads the `HeroArena` and `TeamDeathmatch` logic graphs (the HvV mods' and Instant Action Overhaul V2's):

- **The teams**: team 1's spawns are the light side's, team 2's the dark's, team 0's either's to come back at: a reading of the layers (each of HvV's two four-spawn clusters a side, the many team 0 spawns spread over the ground). Swap them if the graph says otherwise.
- **Geonosis_01's spawns** are all `Enabled: false` in the data (the mode's graph turns them on), so the cut takes every spawn, on or off.
- **Endor_01's Blast** has no spawns and no ground volume, only its two `TeamDeathmatch_Skirmish_DefendAreas`: the ground is the box round both, each side's spawns its area's corners drawn halfway in.
- **Where the grounds stand** (`arenas.js`'s `ARENA_AT`): Hoth's are inside Echo Base under the glacier (the level frame puts them 70 m up on the ice of the site), the other four worlds have no level pack, so each ground's middle goes on an open, level spot of the site: Hoth [320, 20], Endor [−40, 320], Tatooine [220, 100], Geonosis [110, −360], Kashyyyk [60, −400].
- **HvV, the game as played**: a target a side, drawn at the start and again when one falls; only a target's death scores; **10 points** win; 4 a side; 10 s to come back; 10 s out of the arena and you're down.
- **HvV, the bots' tuning** (measured, not the game's): a saber stroke takes 150 of a hero's health, lands 0.35 s into it; a blaster bot fires a burst at its gun's rate but no more often than every 1.2 s (the guns' trigger rates are a player's: Lando's 400 a minute out-shot everyone), hits 75 % point blank to 30 % at 60 m, a saber hero turns 70 % of what it sees; one of yours takes 100; the enemy's target counts 40 m nearer when a bot picks its mark. The heroes' health, healing and guns are the rulebooks' (`bf2017Abilities.json`, `heroes.json`'s regen, `weapons.json`).
- **Blast, the game as played**: **100 kills**, 10 a side (the tier's soldiers cap it). Tuning: a soldier's hit takes 14 more than the assault's (three hits down, not four), they roam within 0.3 of the ground's size round the point halfway between the two sides' spawns, and come back at the level's spawn nearest that point with no enemy within 30 m.

## `spawnLogic` (in `maps/<world>.json`: the Galactic Assault maps' spawn switches, `scripts/lib/bf2017-rulebook-spawnlogic.mjs`)

Read, not by hand: every spawn point's and spawn area's `Enabled` is a property connection out of the mode sub-world's logic records, and the rulebook keeps that graph back from each record to its leaves (bf2-re findings/55); `lib/battlefront/spawnLogic.js` runs it. On Hoth: one area a side for the round's first 25 s (or until the Empire enters either approach box), then eight steps as the lead walker passes its path's waypoint triggers (`PF_EscortTracker`), the hangars' sets in stage 2 (a gate opened at its panel gives the Empire the points inside it; a point the Empire holds closes the Rebels' points by it), the fuel rooms in stage 3 (a spent charge closes the points by it). What the page supplies by hand:

- **Which prefab output means what** (`ROLES` in the reader): the mode prefab's stage ports (`base ^ (ord('1') ^ ord(stage digit))`) and stage-changed event, the tracker's lead, a capture point's owner being Team 2 (`PF_CapturePoint`'s Math node over its `WSCapturePoint` owner), an interact objective's `IsDestroyed`, a gate's opened event. Each was read inside its prefab; the other prefab ports a graph reads are kept with a `null` role and stay false.
- **A capture point's prefab is the stage file's capture volume within 40 m** (`PREFAB_REACH`): the stage file names volumes, the graph prefabs.
- **A geometry trigger fires for a soldier not of its `TeamOfAllies`** inside its linked shape; what `Include ATPlayers` narrows is not read.
- **Not run**: `FollowWaypoints`, `Timeline`, `DynamicQueryFilter`/`QueryCache` (Kamino's and Geonosis's head-counts), `EventAndGate` (each level's `unsupported` lists them: inert in the run). The gates' opened event never fires on the page yet: no one opens a gate's panel.

## The other Galactic Assault maps’ stages (`maps/{endor,tatooine,geonosis,kashyyyk,yavin,kamino,deathstar02}.stages.json`)

Each map’s stage order is a logic graph, as Hoth’s is. What these files take from the data: the stages’ count and order as the level’s own stage strings number them (`ID_FANTASYBATTLES_<LEVEL>_STAGE<n>_TEAM<k>`; Geonosis’s `ID_FANTASYBATTLE_GEONOSIS_{REP,CIS}_PHASE_<n>`), each team’s line in English. By hand:

- **`attackers`**: the side whose first stage line does not defend (`DEFEND`, `STOP`, `HOLD`, `KEEP`, `FEND OFF`, `TAKE OUT THE` the escorted vehicles): Endor, Geonosis and the Death Star II the light side; Tatooine, Kashyyyk, Yavin and Kamino the dark. It agrees with the game.
- **`objectives`: empty.** No route plays these maps’ Galactic Assault yet; the lane that does chooses each stage’s capture volumes, prefabs and spawns from the map rulebook as Hoth’s were chosen (its section above), not this lane.
- Naboo_01 has no stage strings of its own (its Galactic Assault layer names only the planetary missions’), so it has no file.

## Every map rulebook (`maps/<world>[.<n>].json`, the sixth design’s lane maps)

`node scripts/bf2017-data.mjs map --root lab/assets/bf2017 --level <key>` for each of the 44 usable maps (`scripts/lib/bf2017-map-audit.mjs`’s `USABLE`): `hoth_02` → `maps/hoth.2.json`, a campaign map `a1_m0lib_ds02` → `maps/a1_m0lib_ds02.json`. The mode layers are the game’s own ids (`UI/Data/GameModes/<id>`’s `GameModeId` and Aurebesh name): `Domination` is Strike (its prefabs `PF_Strike_Bombs`, `PF_Strike_CTF`, `Pf_FlagDropOff`), `Mode1` Supremacy, `Mode3` Ewok Hunt, `Mode5` and the Strike/Extraction maps’ `Extraction` Extraction, `Mode6` Hero Showdown, `Mode9` the co-op missions, `ModeC` Jetpack Cargo, `Mode7` Hero Starfighters. A campaign map’s rulebook is its mission’s `ObjectivesDefinition` tree (`objectives`), its manifest’s vehicle spawns and terrain; the front end’s two stages have none.

## The other modes (`src/lib/battlefront/modes/strike.js`, `extraction.js`, `ewokHunt.js`, `supremacy.js`; lane 6)

## The way to a far place (`lib/battlefront/navField.js`)

The keypad's place, its reach (the keypad's 1 m and 360° over the button's own 2 m and 90°, on the button's 3× transform), one holder at a time, a let-go starting over, the 2 s arm and 3 s defuse (`GLOBAL_BOMB_INTERACT_TIME_OFF`, `_ON`), the 45 s fuse (`GLOBAL_BOMB_DETONATE_TIME`), the fuse held while a defender holds the keypad and reset by a defuse, the keypad 5 s after the stage switches the charges on, the 0.1 s from a finished hold to its event (the button's `EventMemory`, its events named by their hashes and handled as the image's 0x140ae4f10 does: the hold sends the start of the one who began it, once, and a let-go loses it), the keypad's sound while it is held (the button's `SW02_Gamemodes_Shared_Interaction_01`, its attenuation out to 31.4 m), the explosion's sound 1.33 s before it goes, the names and the three detonations that end the round are the records'. A charge's row keeps its own place, label, switch, keypad and round and names its prefab (`rules`); the prefab's rules are kept once in the map row's `chargeRules` (`charges.js` `chargeOf` puts them together). By hand, or read rather than stated:

- **The reach is the distance times the transform's scale** (1 m × 3 = 3 m), as for the gates' panels, measured from the soldier's feet to the keypad's button 1.58 m up (`charges.js` `canUse`; the gates' `canUse` does the same): so from the floor 2.5 m out.
- **Who arms**: the keypad's `AttackingTeam` is fed twice by the core, its own `TeamId Team2` and the charge's `AttackingTeam`, which Hoth leaves unset; the reader takes the constant. The site gives the arm to the stage's attackers and the defuse to its defenders (`'attack'`, `'defend'`), which on Hoth are Team2 and Team1 as the records have them.
- **The BB units' half hold** (the keypad's `iff(b, t / 2, t)` on BB-8's and BB-9E's ability states) is read into `interact.fast` and not played: the site has neither hero.
- **The markers** (`marker.blast` 5 s, `marker.linger` 3 s) are read and not drawn; the `warn` event (the explosion's sound) is sent out and not yet played.
- **The bots**: the commander sends a squad to an unarmed charge's keypad (the attackers' `take`) and the defenders to an armed one; a bot stands on the navmesh's nearest floor to the keypad that reaches it (`keypadStand`: within the reach, else twice it) and holds from within the AutoPlayers' `InteractionStartRadius` 1 m of it, as at a gate's panel. The game's automation (`Automation_ArmObjective`, a test teleporting a player to the button and pressing the interact 4.5 s) is a test's, not the bots'.
- **Sight and bolts under the mountain**: the sim's line test (`nav.js` `firstSolid`: the bots' sight, their fire, every bolt) took the 2 m grid's terrain for the ground, and over the fuel rooms that is the snow 75 m up: nothing in Echo Base saw or hit anything. Under the terrain a point is now open where the battle's navmesh certifies the air: within its agent's height (1.8 m) over a polygon, or its radius (0.4 m) past one's rim (`meshNav.js` `attachMesh`'s `nav.open`; findings/57 §2 has the agent). That NavPower's baked agent column is free of the level is how the bake works; that a line is stopped wherever the mesh has no floor under it (rock, a machine, a ceiling over 1.8 m up) is the site's reading, and a wall too thin to cut the mesh does not stop it. The game's own line of fire is a physics ray (findings/28 §6). A hook into main's `nav.js` and `meshNav.js`.
- **The bots hear the keypad**: while one holds a keypad, the holder's enemies whose eyes are within the sound's 31.4 m of the button believe in the holder (`modes/galacticAssault.js` `sounds`, a stimulus to the senses in `ai/bots.js`), and turn on them. That the curve's end is how far a bot hears it, that it is believed where the holder stands rather than at the button (a bot turned to the keypad has its back to a holder at its shoulder: both sides' bots stand at the one floor nearest the keypad), and at the site's heard-belief confidence, are the site's: the game's Galactic Assault has no bots. Before these two the bots of both sides stood at the keypad unseen and unhurt, and a charge could go armed, defused and re-armed every 10 s for the rest of the round (findings/63 fix round 1); after them, in seven bots-only runs of stage 3 from different starts, no charge was armed more than three times and six rounds ended in the attackers’ win (340 to 631 s), the seventh still held by the defenders at 895 s.
- **The pickups** (`PF_Pickup_ObjectiveBomb` ×11) are the Rebels' HH-12 ion disruptors, not a charge to carry: no charge needs one to arm (findings/63 §6). They are not on the site yet.
- **The other maps**: Kashyyyk's four, Tatooine's one and Yavin's three charges are the same prefab and are read into their maps' rows the same way (each level wires only the locator, `Enabled` and `ObjectiveName` into a charge; anything more would be in the row's `unread`); no stage file plays them yet. How many must go is read only where the detonations go into an `EventAndGate` (Hoth's and Yavin's 3); Kashyyyk's go into a logic prefab each and Tatooine's into a splitter, and their `win` is `null`.
